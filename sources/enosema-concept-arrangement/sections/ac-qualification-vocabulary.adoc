== Relationship qualification vocabulary

This annex states the controlled vocabulary that qualification
metadata uses (Q-12). The register declares extensions per EFS 2513,
clause 5; undeclared values do not appear in published records.

=== Presence constraint (partitive relations)

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Value
|Meaning

|`obligatory`
|every instance of the whole has the part

|`optional`
|instances of the whole may lack the part
|===

=== Count constraint (partitive relations)

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Value
|Meaning

|`n`
|exactly n parts

|`n..m`
|between n and m parts

|`one-or-more`
|at least one part

|`zero-or-more`
|zero or more parts

|`unknown`
|the definitions do not fix a count; the constraint is not asserted
|===

=== Normative force

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Value
|Meaning

|`essential`
|the relation holds for every instance of the concepts under their
definitions

|`characteristic`
|the relation holds as the definitions characterize the concepts but
is not necessary for every instance

|`contextual`
|the relation holds under the conditions stated in the qualification
note
|===

=== Lifecycle status

[cols="2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Value
|Meaning

|`proposed`
|asserted in a proposal; not public in the arrangement view

|`confirmed`
|accepted through review; public (Q-3)

|`deprecated`
|retired; removed from the arrangement view; qualification retained
(Q-10)
|===

=== Associative connection terms

The following connection terms annotate associative relations
(E-9, Q-7). The set follows the associative relation practice of
ISO 12620 data categories and the ES relation vocabulary (EFS 2510);
the register declares further terms as extensions.

`causes`:: the subject concept brings about the object concept.

`is-produced-by`:: the subject concept results from the object
concept.

`produces`:: inverse of `is-produced-by`.

`is-prerequisite-for`:: the object concept presupposes the subject
concept.

`is-instrument-for`:: the subject concept is used in realizing the
object concept.

`opposes`:: the concepts are contrastive in the domain.

`interacts-with`:: the concepts stand in a thematic connection not
covered by the terms above; the qualification note states the
connection.

=== Mapping degree

The degree values of mapping relations are the mapping relation types
of EFS 2510: `equivalence`, `narrower`, `broader`, `overlap`,
`dissociation`. The difference note is required for every non-
equivalent degree (Q-8).

=== Criterion naming

The criterion of subdivision is qualification metadata (Q-2), and its
name is part of the published record. A criterion is named well when
the name passes three checks:

* *it names a characteristic family*: `currency`, `content type`,
`generation mechanism` — not `type`, `category`, `other`;

* *it names the dimension, not a value*: the rake's criterion is
`currency`, and `current` is a coordinate value, not a criterion; and

* *it is stable across the rake*: the same name appears on every
relation of the rake (T2) and in the system description's dimension
declaration (A-10).

A criterion name that fails the checks is a finding under T2 and is
fixed by renaming the criterion in the system description and on the
relations, which is a change-management event when it alters what
consumers see (EFS 2513, clause 7).

== MECE test suite

This annex states the MECE tests of clause <<mece>> in executable
form: each test names its trigger, its inputs, its procedure, and its
failure output. Tests marked *(mechanical)* run over the published
relation records without expert judgement (M-8); tests marked *(review)*
are procedures for reviewers and produce findings through the issue
resolution of EFS 2513, clause 9.

=== T1: rake enumeration *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* M-11.

*Procedure:* group the hierarchical relations of the system by
superordinate concept; each group is a rake; verify that every
non-root concept appears in exactly one rake and that every rake
carries a criterion value on each of its relations.

*Failure output:* concepts in no rake, concepts in several rakes,
relations without criterion.

=== T2: single criterion *(mechanical where criteria are structured)*

*Requirement:* M-3.

*Procedure:* for each rake, read the criterion value of its
relations; verify that the rake carries one criterion.

*Failure output:* a rake with relations carrying different criteria.
Where criteria are free statements, the test degrades to a review
check (T3).

=== T3: intensional disjointness *(review)*

*Requirement:* M-1.

*Procedure:* for each pair of coordinates in the rake, attempt to
construct a characteristic set that satisfies both intensions. Record
the construction that fails. The pair passes when the reviewer can
state the reason no construction exists — a contradiction between
characteristics, a boundary convention, or a delimiting pair.

*Failure output:* the pair, the attempted constructions, and the
finding that no separating argument was produced.

=== T4: extensional disjointness *(review; mechanical where boundaries
are structured)*

*Requirement:* M-2.

*Procedure:* for each pair of coordinates, read the separating
evidence: the delimiting characteristic pair, the measured boundary,
or the assignment convention. Verify that the evidence assigns every
object to one coordinate.

*Failure output:* pairs whose evidence is absent or does not
partition.

=== T5: boundary convention *(mechanical where ranges are
structured)*

*Requirement:* M-7.

*Procedure:* for rakes of ranged concepts, read the boundary values
of the coordinates; verify that adjacent ranges share no open value
and skip no value.

*Failure output:* overlapping boundaries, gapped boundaries.

=== T6: coverage *(review)*

*Requirement:* M-5.

*Procedure:* read the coverage statement of the rake: the derivation,
the source, or the residual marker. Verify that the statement exists
and that its content is checkable — a derivation names the
characteristics that partition, a source cites a document, a residual
marker names the residual coordinate.

*Failure output:* rakes without coverage statements.

=== T7: residual flag *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* M-6.

*Procedure:* detect residual categories in rakes; verify that each is
declared as a limitation in the system description.

*Failure output:* undeclared residual categories.

=== T8: uniform criterion expression *(mechanical where designations
are structured)*

*Requirement:* M-4.

*Procedure:* where the criterion is expressed in designations, verify
that the coordinate designations express it in a uniform form, per
the consistency criteria of EFS 2522, Group S.

*Failure output:* coordinates whose designations express the
criterion differently.

=== T9: duplicate placement *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* A-6, E-7.

*Procedure:* verify that each concept has at most one incoming
hierarchical relation in the system, and that no direct and
transitive placement of the same pair exists.

*Failure output:* multiply placed concepts, redundant paths.

=== T10: cross-system consistency *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* V-6, A-11.

*Procedure:* for each cross-system membership, verify that the mapped
positions correspond under the asserted mapping relations and that
each mapping relation carries its degree.

*Failure output:* memberships without mappings, mappings without
degree.

=== T11: cycle detection *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* A-6, E-7.

*Procedure:* traverse the hierarchical relations of the system from
each root; verify that the traversal is acyclic — no concept is its
own ancestor through the asserted relations.

*Failure output:* cycles, stated as the concept path that closes.

=== T12: orphan detection *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* A-8.

*Procedure:* identify concepts whose membership relations in the
system are all deprecated and which carry no confirmed relation;
verify that each such concept is either absent from the arrangement
view or carries an open placement need.

*Failure output:* orphans published in the arrangement view; orphans
without placement needs.

=== T13: depth profile *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* the depth and breadth practice of <<arrangement>>.

*Procedure:* compute the depth of each root-to-leaf path and the size
of each rake; flag single-child subdivisions and rakes exceeding the
charter's breadth guideline.

*Failure output:* single-child subdivisions, oversized rakes. The
test is advisory: its output feeds the review agenda and the
re-dimension decisions of M-9, and does not block publication.

=== T14: mapping symmetry *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* A-11, E-10, Q-8.

*Procedure:* for each cross-system membership pair, verify that the
mapping relations asserted in the two systems agree in degree — a
membership mapped as `equivalence` in one system and as `overlap` in
the other is a defect — and that non-equivalent mappings carry
difference notes.

*Failure output:* asymmetric mappings, mappings without difference
notes.

=== T15: qualification completeness *(mechanical)*

*Requirement:* Q-1 to Q-5, Q-12.

*Procedure:* verify that every confirmed hierarchical relation
carries source, criterion, normative force and, for partitive
relations, presence and count constraints with values from the
declared vocabulary (Annex C).

*Failure output:* relations with missing or undeclared qualification
values.

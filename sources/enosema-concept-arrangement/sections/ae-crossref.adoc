the requirements of the other ES terminology documents. The map is
informative; the normative texts are the cited clauses.

=== Identifiers in other documents

The requirement identifiers of this document are stable and are cited
elsewhere:

* EFG 2519 cites Group A, Group E, Group Q, Group M and Group V in the
self-assessment checklist of its Annex C; and

* EFS 2522 cites Group M in the consistency dimension (Group S) of its
scoring model, where an entry inherits a finding from a rake that
fails the MECE tests.

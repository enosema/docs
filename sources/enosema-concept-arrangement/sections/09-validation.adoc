[[validation]]
== Arrangement validation (Group V)

=== General

Validation keeps the arrangement true after the day it was built.
Concepts move, criteria are renamed, systems merge, and each event can
break a property that held when the relations were last reviewed.
Group V states what is checked, by what means, how often, and what
the checks produce.

=== Mechanical validation

V-1:: The register shall validate the mechanical properties of the
arrangement as part of publication validation (EFS 2513, clause 10):

* every non-root concept of each system has exactly one incoming
hierarchical relation within the system (A-6);

* every rake is enumerable from the relation records, and every rake
carries its criterion of subdivision (A-15, Q-2);

* every hierarchical relation carries source, criterion and status
(Q-1 to Q-3);

* no direct and transitive placement of the same pair exists (E-7);
and

* no duplicate assertion of one relation type between one pair of
concepts in one system exists (E-16).

V-2:: The register shall validate the mechanical subset of the MECE
tests at the same gate (M-8): intensional-disjointness checks that
run on structured characteristic data, boundary-convention presence
checks for rakes of ranged concepts, and residual-category flagging.

V-3:: A validation failure blocks publication of the affected system
view until remediated or waived by the control body (M-8). Waivers
are recorded with reasons and carry an expiry: a waived failure is
re-tested at the next scheduled review (EFG 2519, clause 12).

=== Review validation

V-4:: The register shall review the non-mechanical properties of the
arrangement on a schedule and on events:

* on a scheduled arrangement review, at the cadence the charter sets
(EFS 2513, clause 11), a sample of rakes is tested under the full MECE
suite (Annex B) and a sample of relationships is re-qualified against
its recorded source (Q-1); and

* on events that can invalidate properties — a definition change, a
system merge or split, a criterion rename, a system description
change — the affected rakes and relationships are queued for review
before the next publication (Q-11).

V-5:: The review of an arrangement uses the placement decision
procedure of Annex A as the review instrument: a reviewer re-derives
the position of a concept from its intension and the rake criterion
and compares the derivation with the recorded position. Divergence is
a finding, and the finding follows the issue resolution of EFS 2513,
clause 9.

V-6:: A review shall test cross-system consistency where cross-system
membership exists (A-11): the mapped positions shall correspond under
the mapping relations asserted between the systems, and the mapping
relations shall carry their degree and difference notes (Q-8).

=== Sampling for review

The scheduled review (V-4) does not re-derive every position in a
large register, and it does not need to: the mechanical tests already
cover what is mechanical, and the review effort concentrates where
judgement carries the property. The register samples:

* *by risk*: rakes with residual categories, rakes remediated within
the period, rakes whose relations changed, and systems under active
extension are sampled first;

* *by age*: relationships whose last review predates the charter
cadence are sampled proportionally to their age; and

* *by coverage*: over a bounded number of review cycles, every rake of
every system is visited at least once — the sampling is stratified to
converge, so that no rake is exempt indefinitely.

The sample size and the stratification are charter decisions (EFS
2513, clause 4); the requirement is convergence plus risk-weighting,
not a fixed percentage.

=== Validation record format

The record of a validation run (V-7) carries, at minimum:

----
validation:
  run: 2026-11-05T09:00Z
  systems: [data-resource-system, statistics-metadata-system]
  mechanical:
    tests: [T1, T2, T5, T7, T8, T9, T10]
    rakes_checked: 214
    failures: []
  waivers:
    - rake: delivery-time
      test: T5
      reason: boundary convention pending decision 2026-12-01
      expires: 2026-12-01
  review:
    rakes_sampled: 12
    findings: 2
    finding_ids: [EFSD-0031, EFSD-0032]
  publication:
    blocked: false
----

The format is illustrative; the requirement is the content set. A
record that omits the waivers or the findings is an incomplete record
under EFS 2513, clause 10, and the register's publication artifact
carries the validation summary so that consumers can see the state of
the arrangement they are reading.

=== Evidence of validation

V-7:: Each validation run shall produce a record that states: the
systems checked, the rakes tested, the tests applied, the failures
found, the waivers granted, and the remediations applied. The record
is a register record under EFS 2513, clause 10, and is discoverable
with the register publication.

V-8:: The arrangement quality of a register is observable from its
validation records over time: the count of failing rakes, the count
of waived failures, the count of re-qualified relationships, and the
count of placement changes. EFG 2519, clause 12, consumes these
figures in the improvement practice of a conforming concept
management system.

[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviated terms

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions
apply.

[[term-concept]]
==== concept

unit of knowledge created by a unique combination of characteristics

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-characteristic]]
==== characteristic

abstraction of a property of an object or of a set of objects

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-essential-characteristic]]
==== essential characteristic

characteristic of a concept that is required by the concept's
intension

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-delimiting-characteristic]]
==== delimiting characteristic

essential characteristic that distinguishes a concept from its
coordinate concepts

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-intension]]
==== intension

set of characteristics that makes up the concept

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-extension]]
==== extension

set of objects to which the concept applies

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-concept-system]]
==== concept system

set of concepts structured according to the relations among them

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-concept-arrangement]]
==== concept arrangement

assignment of a set of concepts to positions in a concept system,
together with the relations that hold the positions together

[[term-criterion-of-subdivision]]
==== criterion of subdivision

criterion of categorization

single essential characteristic applied to subdivide the extension of
a superordinate concept into its coordinate subordinate concepts

[SOURCE: ISO 704, modified — the established term "criterion of
subdivision" is retained for this document]

[[term-dimension]]
==== dimension

criterion of subdivision that is declared, named and applied
consistently across the coordinate concepts of a subdivision

[[term-multidimensional-concept-system]]
==== multidimensional concept system

concept system in which the coordinate subordinate concepts of a
superordinate concept are produced by subdividing along several
declared dimensions simultaneously and are composed into the positions
of the system

[SOURCE: ISO 704, modified — expressed as a requirement-bearing
system shape]

[[term-concept-position]]
==== concept position

location of a concept in a concept system, determined by the concept's
superordinate concept and by the criterion of subdivision that
separates it from its coordinate concepts

[[term-system-membership]]
==== system membership

recorded assertion that a concept occupies a concept position in a
stated concept system

[[term-mutual-exclusivity]]
==== mutual exclusivity

property of a subdivision under which no concept satisfies the
intension of more than one coordinate subordinate concept of the
subdivision

[[term-collective-exhaustiveness]]
==== collective exhaustiveness

property of a subdivision under which the extension of the
superordinate concept is covered by the union of the extensions of its
coordinate subordinate concepts

[[term-residual-category]]
==== residual category

coordinate subordinate concept whose extension is defined as the part
of the superordinate extension not covered by the other coordinate
concepts

[[term-relationship-establishment]]
==== relationship establishment

selection of a relation type from a relation vocabulary, direction of
the relation between its stated ends, and recording of the assertion
in the register

[[term-relationship-qualification]]
==== relationship qualification

association of a recorded relationship with the metadata that states
its evidence and scope: source, criterion, delimitation, presence and
count constraints, normative force, and status

[[term-normative-force]]
==== normative force

weight of a relationship assertion in the arrangement, expressed as
essential, characteristic or contextual

[[term-cross-system-membership]]
==== cross-system membership

system membership of one concept in two or more concept systems that
are maintained independently, with the relation set of each system
recorded separately

[[term-rake]]
==== rake

complete set of coordinate subordinate concepts produced by one
subdivision of a superordinate concept

[[term-generic-relation]]
==== generic relation

relation between two concepts where the intension of one concept
forms part of the intension of the other, and where the extension of
the former includes the extension of the latter

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-partitive-relation]]
==== partitive relation

relation between two concepts where one concept forms the whole and
the other concept forms a part of that whole

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-associative-relation]]
==== associative relation

pragmatic relation

relation between two concepts having a non-hierarchical thematic
connection by virtue of experience

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-mapping-relation]]
==== mapping relation

relation between concepts or concept positions of different concept
systems that states the degree of their correspondence

[[term-superordinate-concept]]
==== superordinate concept

concept whose intension is presupposed by the intension of another
concept and whose extension includes the extension of that other
concept

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-subordinate-concept]]
==== subordinate concept

concept whose intension presupposes the intension of another concept
and whose extension is included in the extension of that other
concept

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-coordinate-concept]]
==== coordinate concept

concept that shares the superordinate concept of another concept and
differs from it by the delimiting characteristics of one criterion of
subdivision

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1, modified — bound to the single criterion of
this document]

[[term-generic-concept-system]]
==== generic concept system

concept system structured predominantly by generic relations

[[term-partitive-concept-system]]
==== partitive concept system

concept system structured predominantly by partitive relations

[[term-placeholder-superordinate]]
==== placeholder superordinate

superordinate concept introduced to hold a rake for which no natural
superordinate concept exists, declared as such in the system
description

[[term-orphan]]
==== orphan

concept whose membership relations in a system are all deprecated
without replacement

=== Abbreviated terms

MECE::
mutual exclusivity, collective exhaustiveness — the paired property
that a subdivision is required to satisfy under this document

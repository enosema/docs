== Foreword

ES is the publishing imprint of the Enosema Foundation. ES documents

This document was prepared by Technical Committee Terminology.

This first edition of EFS 2525 establishes the requirements for
arranging concepts into concept systems: how a concept becomes a member
of one or more concept systems, how the relations that position a
concept in a system are established, how those relations are qualified
with the metadata that reviewers and consumers need, and how the
mutual exclusivity and collective exhaustiveness of every subdivision
is established and maintained.

This document is one of the Terminology series of ES. It builds on the
relation vocabulary of EFS 2510, the concept management system
requirements of EFS 2513, the interchange record of EFS 2516, and the
entry quality requirements of EFS 2522. Where this document states a
requirement about relation types, relation records, or entry records,
the vocabulary and the record structure of those documents apply.

The requirements of this document are grouped into five groups with
stable identifiers: Group A (system membership), Group E (relationship
establishment), Group Q (relationship qualification), Group M (mutual
exclusivity and exhaustiveness), and Group V (arrangement validation).
Requirement identifiers are stable: they do not change between
editions, and cross-references in conformity assessments, checklists,
and tooling cite the identifiers rather than clause numbers.

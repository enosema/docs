[[qualifying]]
== Relationship qualification (Group Q)

=== General

An assertion states that a relation holds. A qualification states why
it holds, how far it holds, and how confident the register is that it
holds. The distinction matters because consumers act on relations: a
partitive relation with a count constraint tells a data modeller that
a fixed number of parts exist; a generic relation with a cited source
tells a reviewer where the position came from; a status marks an
assertion that review has or has not confirmed. Group Q states the
metadata that every hierarchical relationship carries, the additional
metadata that specific relation types require, and the vocabulary for
stating it.

=== Required qualification metadata

Q-1:: Every hierarchical relationship shall carry a source: a citation
of the document, proposal, decision record, or review action from
which the position derives. Where the position derives from this
register's own review, the source cites the decision record (EFG 2519,
Annex B).

Q-2:: Every hierarchical relationship shall carry the criterion of
subdivision under which the subordinate concept is coordinate with its
rake (A-2). The criterion is stated as a named dimension where the
system declares dimensions, and as a free statement where it does not.

Q-3:: Every hierarchical relationship shall carry a status from the
lifecycle of EFS 2513, clause 8: `proposed`, `confirmed`, or
`deprecated`. A relation becomes public only at status `confirmed`.

Q-4:: A hierarchical relationship shall carry its normative force:
*essential*, where the relation holds for every instance of the
concepts under their definitions; *characteristic*, where the relation
holds in the way the definitions characterize the concepts but is not
necessary to every instance; or *contextual*, where the relation holds
under conditions that the qualification states.

=== Type-specific qualification

Q-5:: A partitive relationship shall state the presence constraint
(`obligatory` or `optional`) and, where the definitions fix it, the
count constraint: a number, a range, or an open constraint
(`one-or-more`, `zero-or-more`). The constraint follows the
multiplicity of ISO 704 part-whole analysis and is recorded per EFS
2516 relation fields.

Q-6:: A partitive relationship shall state the delimitation of the
part: the criterion that separates the part from the other parts of
the same whole, so that the parts of the whole are themselves mutually
exclusive under Group M.

Q-7:: An associative relationship shall state the thematic connection
that motivates it (E-9), using a connection term from the ES relation
vocabulary where one exists and a stated phrase where none does.

Q-8:: A mapping relationship shall state the degree of the
correspondence using the mapping types of EFS 2510 and, for
non-equivalent correspondences, a note that states what differs and
for whom (EFS 2504, equivalence notes).

=== Status and lifecycle of qualification

Q-9:: The status of a relationship shall change only through the
change management requirements of EFS 2513, clause 7: a proposal
asserts the relation at `proposed`; review sets it to `confirmed`;
deprecation retires it while keeping it visible.

Q-10:: A deprecated relationship shall retain its full qualification.
Deprecation removes the relation from the arrangement view; it does
not remove the evidence that once justified the position, which
remains load-bearing for the history of the system.

Q-11:: Where a relationship's qualification becomes stale — the
criterion is renamed, the source document is withdrawn, the count
constraint is contradicted by a revision — the register shall raise a
review task for the relationship under the cadence of EFS 2513,
clause 11, and shall not silently inherit the stale qualification.

=== Qualification records

The qualification of a relationship is a set of fields on the relation
record (EFS 2516). The following worked records show the shape for the
three families that carry or annotate arrangement. The records are
illustrative; field names follow the interchange record of EFS 2516.

*A generic relation with full qualification:*

----
relation:
  type: generic
  subject: data-set:current-data-set
  object: data-set
  system: data-resource-system
  qualification:
    criterion: currency
    normative_force: essential
    source: EFS-DM-0012, decision 2026-04-07
    status: confirmed
----

The record states where the concept sits, under what criterion, on
what evidence, and at what weight. A consumer can test the position:
the criterion names the rake, the source names the decision, and the
force states that the relation is definitional.

*A partitive relation with constraints and delimitation:*

----
relation:
  type: partitive
  subject: data-set:header-section
  object: data-set
  system: data-resource-system
  qualification:
    presence: obligatory
    count: "1"
    delimitation: metadata carried at the set level rather than the
      record level
    normative_force: essential
    source: EFS-DM-0015, review 2026-06-12
    status: confirmed
----

The record tells a data modeller that the part is mandatory and
single-valued, and tells a reviewer what separates this part from the
other parts of the whole — the input that test T4 consumes.

*An associative relation with its connection stated:*

----
relation:
  type: associative
  subject: data-set:header-section
  object: data-set:validation-rules
  system: data-resource-system
  qualification:
    connection: is-prerequisite-for
    note: header section carries the identifiers that the validation
      rules reference
    normative_force: characteristic
    source: proposal EFS-DM-0021, accepted 2026-08-30
    status: confirmed
----

The record annotates the arrangement without carrying placement
weight (E-8), and the note states the connection specifically enough
to test.

=== Qualification quality

Qualification is itself subject to quality expectations, and the
register reviews it as such:

* a source that names no document, decision or review is not a source
(Q-1): "`agreed in discussion`" does not qualify a position, because
no one can re-derive it;

* a criterion that names no dimension and no characteristic is not a
criterion (Q-2): "`general`" and "`other`" fail the test that T2 and
T3 apply;

* a note that states no testable content is not a qualification note
(E-9, Q-8): the note fails when no reviewer action would differ if
the note were absent; and

* the score sheet practice of EFS 2522 applies to qualification
records where a register scores entries: a position with defective
qualification drags the consistency dimension of its entries.

=== Qualification vocabulary

Q-12:: Qualification metadata shall use the vocabulary of Annex C:
presence constraints, count constraints, normative force values,
status values, and connection terms. The register may extend the
vocabulary by declaring additional terms in the system description
(EFS 2513, clause 5); undeclared terms shall not appear in published
records.

Q-13:: Qualification metadata shall be recorded in machine-readable
form per EFS 2516. A qualification that exists only in prose — a note
in a document that the relation record does not carry — is
unqualified for the purposes of this document.

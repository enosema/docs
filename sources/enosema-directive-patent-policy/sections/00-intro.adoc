== Introduction

=== General

ES is committed to expanding the realm of standards for terminology,
concepts, and their management, with a focus on terminology resources
and interoperability across the globe and communities
of practice.

As a proponent of open standards, ES does not endorse
mandatory usage of patents within ES Standards.
The Enosema Board of Directors has decided to align
IPR practice with major international standardization bodies
including ISO, IEC and ITU.

=== Purpose

This document describes the policy of ES with regards to patent
usage within ES deliverables and provides a
"`code of practice`" that is simple and straightforward.

ES deliverables are drawn up by technical, not patent, experts,
who may not be fully aware of all nuances in the complex
international legal situation of intellectual property rights.

ES deliverables are non-binding -- their objective is to ensure
compatibility of technologies and systems on a worldwide basis.

To ensure the applicability and accessibility of ES
deliverables, the implementation and continued usage of content within
the deliverables must be possible to the worldwide audience without
discrimination, including any patent embodiment in such deliverables.

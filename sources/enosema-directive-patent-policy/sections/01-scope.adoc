== Scope

This document provides the ES patent policy, which governs all
standardization deliverables developed through the ES
standardization process.

The ES patent policy is identical to the <<ISOPP>> in content,
except where:

* entity names such as "`ISO`", "`IEC`", "`ITU-T`" and "`ITU-R`" were
changed to "`ES`";

* deliverables and processes pertaining to those entities have been
changed to their counterparts within ES.

Detailed arrangements arising from patents (licensing, royalties, etc.)
are left to the parties concerned, as these arrangements might differ
from case to case.

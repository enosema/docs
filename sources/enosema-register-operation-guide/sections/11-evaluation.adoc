== Evaluation in practice

This clause gives guidance for the requirements of EFS 2513, clause 11.

=== Monitoring

Measure a small number of things that drive action:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Metric
|How to collect
|Action trigger

|Intake time (proposal opened → review started)
|Issue tracker timestamps
|Median exceeds the charter's stated window

|Decision time (review complete → control-body decision)
|Decision record dates
|Median grows across two quarters

|Validation error rate at publication runs
|CI logs
|Any non-zero trend

|Consumer-reported issues
|Issue tracker label
|Any issue touching identifiers or references
|===

A dashboard is built only for numbers that have action triggers: a
metric without a trigger produces no improvement and is omitted from
monitoring.

=== The periodic review

Run the review per concept system at a planned interval (annually is a
workable default; EFS 2513, 11.2). The review is a working session with
a written output, not a ceremony:

. *Run the full validation suite* against the live dataset; log
findings with severity.

. *Sample records for depth checks*: for each criterion of
subdivision, confirm it is still stated on the records it generated
(EFS 2513, 11.2); sample `full`-degree localizations and re-verify the
degree (clause 8).

. *Check lifecycle duality*: retired items resolve; their successor
information is present; register-item and concept-level lifecycle
relations agree.

. *Check the SKOS projection* against the full dataset: no concepts
lost, no semantics altered (EFS 2516, 6.2).

. *Review the context records* of clause 4: purpose sentence, audience
worksheet, boundaries — still true?

The output is a review record: findings, disposition of each, and the
improvement actions raised into the process of clause 12.

=== Internal audit

The audit examines the *system*, not the concepts: are decision
records being written? Were any changes published without decision?
Do role assignments match practice? Is the appeals channel used and
functioning?

A light, effective pattern: once per year, trace five published changes
end-to-end — proposal → review → decision record → publication run →
live record. Every step must be documented and dated. Any gap is a
nonconformity to disposition (clause 12).

=== Self-assessment against EFS 2513

Annex C contains a clause-by-clause checklist: every line names an
EFS 2513 requirement and asks the question an assessor would ask.
Run it:

* before first claiming a requirements class (Annex E of this guide);
* at each internal audit; and
* before each renewal of a public conformance claim (EFS 2513, 11.4).

Record the completed checklist with its date and the assessor; the
claim of conformity then cites it.

=== Sample review record

The periodic review's written output, in the shape this guide expects
(clause 11, EFS 2513, 11.2):

[source,yaml]
----
review:
  register: <dataset-id>
  date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
  interval: annual
  validation:
    suite: full
    findings: 0 errors, 2 warnings (both pre-existing, dispositioned)
  depth_checks:
    criteria_register:
      sampled: 6 criteria
      result: all stated on records they generated
    localizations:
      sampled: 5 full-degree
      reverified: 4 full, 1 reduced to partial (note added, DR-2026-021)
  lifecycle_duality:
    retired_items_checked: 7
    all_resolve_with_successor_info: true
  skos_projection:
    concepts_in_full_dataset: 109
    concepts_in_projection: 109
    semantics_altered: none
  context_records:
    reviewed: true
    changes_needed: none
  findings:
    - finding: "'partial' localization reduced on re-verification"
      disposition: corrected (note added); no sweep needed
  improvements_raised: []
  next_review: <date + interval>
----

=== Audit trace walkthrough

The once-a-year trace of five published changes, spelled out for one
change so the pattern is unambiguous. The auditor picks a published
record and follows it backwards:

. *Live record*: the record on the published site says definition
version B, accepted 2026-11-11.

. *Publication run*: the publication log has an entry on that date;
its trigger cites `merge of PR #12 (decision DR-2026-014)`.

. *Pull request*: PR #12 touches exactly the record and the files the
proposal described; no other records changed.

. *Proposal*: issue #87 carries a complete proposal form (Annex A).

. *Review*: the review comment applies the clause 7 checklists with
one required change and a recommendation.

. *Decision*: DR-2026-014 records the question, the vote (3 yes),
and the rationale, within the charter's window.

. *Localization trigger*: the record has localizations; the change
touched the intension; review tasks were opened per clause 8 (or the
audit records the gap).

Every arrow resolved with a dated record: the change passes. Any arrow
that dead-ends — an undocumented merge, a missing decision record, a
localization that should have been re-reviewed and was not — is a
nonconformity with a clause number attached.

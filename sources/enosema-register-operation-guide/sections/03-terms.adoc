[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the terms and definitions given in
EFS 2510, EFS 2513 and EFS 2516 apply.

This guide additionally uses the following working terms.

[[term-intake]]
==== intake

first handling of a proposal: completeness check, triage, and
assignment to a reviewer, before any technical judgement is made

[[term-decision-record]]
==== decision record

controlled record of one decision of the control body: what was
proposed, what was decided, why, and on whose vote

[[term-publication-run]]
==== publication run

one execution of the register publication procedure: validation,
build, checks, and release of the register's published artifacts

[[term-register-edition]]
==== register edition

a published, immutable state of the register, identified by year or
version, whose records do not change after publication

[[term-submission-channel]]
==== submission channel

the mechanism by which proposals reach the register, typically the
issue tracker of the register's source repository

== Self-assessment checklist against EFS 2513

Run this checklist per register. For each line: mark Conforming (C),
Partial (P), or Nonconforming (N); for P and N, record the finding and
the action. The completed checklist, dated and signed, is the record
that supports a conformance claim (EFS 2513, 11.4).

=== Scoring guidance

* *C on all lines of a class* → the class may be claimed (Annex E).

* *P on any line* → claim only with a recorded remediation plan and
date; auditors treat stale P entries as N.

* *N on any line of a claimed class* → the claim is withdrawn or
narrowed until the finding is dispositioned; publishing a claim with a
known N is the fastest way to lose the trust the register exists to
earn.

=== Assessment record template

The completed assessment is itself a controlled record:

[source,yaml]
----
assessment:
  register: <dataset-id>
  date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
  assessor: <name, role>
  classes_assessed: [governed]      # per the adoption worksheet, Annex E
  lines_total: 46
  conforming: 44
  partial: 2
  nonconforming: 0
  partial_findings:
    - line: "8.2-1"
      finding: >-
        Conversion system field recorded for romanizations, but not
        yet validated automatically at publication.
      action: add localization field validation to CI
      target_date: <date>
  nonconforming_findings: []
  claim:
    statement: >-
      This register claims conformity to EFS 2513 requirements class
      "governed" on the basis of this assessment.
    valid_until: <next review date>
----

Keep every completed assessment; the sequence of assessments is the
register's conformity history, and improvement decisions (clause 12)
cite it.

== Introduction

=== Why an operation guide

EFS 2513 specifies what a conforming concept management system does. It
says that a register shall have a control body, that changes shall go
through proposal and review, that records shall be machine-readable,
that localizations shall carry equivalence degrees. It does not state
how an organization establishes and operates those arrangements in
daily practice.

This guide fills that gap. It is to EFS 2513 what ISO 9004 is to
ISO 9001: guidance for organizations that intend to implement, operate,
and steadily improve a concept register, written from practice rather
than from principle. Every clause of this guide corresponds to a clause
of EFS 2513, and its annexes contain the working artifacts an
organization needs — proposal forms, decision records, checklists, and
a complete worked example from a register that ES itself operates.

=== How to use this guide

Reading paths by situation:

* *Starting a register*: read clauses 4 to 7 in order, then Annex D
(the worked example), and adopt Annex A (proposal form) and Annex B
(decision record) as the first working documents of the register.

* *Achieving conformity with EFS 2513*: apply Annex C (the
self-assessment checklist) to current practice; each line cites the
EFS 2513 requirement that it examines. Clause 11 explains how to
run the assessment and what to record.

* *Improving an existing register*: run the assessment first, then
apply clause 12 (improvement practice) to the findings.

* *Extending an existing system to localization*: clause 8.

* *Extending an existing system to a public, machine-readable
publication*: clauses 7 (publication runbook), 9 (relation practice),
and 10 (tooling), with EFS 2516 alongside.

=== Conventions

* The word *should* marks recommended practice; an organization may
deviate, and records the deviation and its reason.

* Templates in annexes are starting points: adapt names, fields, and
wording to the organization, and keep the structure.

* Examples marked as coming from the ES Relation Vocabulary are real:
the dataset is published by ES at the Enosema concept register, and its
files are cited verbatim.

* Guidance that corresponds to a specific EFS 2513 requirement is
cross-referenced inline in the form (EFS 2513, «clause»), so the two
documents can be read side by side.

== Scope

This guide describes practice for establishing, operating, evaluating,
and improving a concept register — the register-facing part of a concept
management system as specified in EFS 2513.

It gives guidance, templates, checklists, and worked examples for:

* scoping a register and analysing its audiences (clause 4);

* establishing and operating governance: control bodies, decision
records, appeals (clause 5);

* planning identifiers, editions, and concept system structure
(clause 6);

* day-to-day concept operations: intake, review, acceptance, change,
retirement, and publication (clause 7);

* localization practice (clause 8);

* relation management practice (clause 9);

* tooling, automation, and repository practice (clause 10);

* evaluation and improvement practice (clauses 11 and 12); and

* working artifacts: proposal form, decision record, self-assessment
checklist, a complete worked example, and an adoption worksheet
(annexes A to E).

This guide is informative. It places no requirements; EFS 2513 places
the requirements, and this guide suggests ways to meet them. An
organization is free to follow different practice and still conform to
EFS 2513, provided the requirements themselves are met.

This guide does not supersede EFD 101 (standardization and publication
processes) or EFD 102 (standard document requirements); ES's own
documents follow those directives, and this guide's examples are drawn
from practice conducted under them.

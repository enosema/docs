== Worked example — the ES Relation Vocabulary

This annex walks one real register end to end: the ES Relation
Vocabulary, the controlled vocabulary of concept relation types defined
in EFS 2510, clause 6, published by ES at the Enosema concept register.
Everything below is taken from the register as it operates.

=== Purpose and audience (clause 4)

*Purpose sentence*: "`The register provides the authoritative set of
concept relation types used in ES concept systems and exchanged between
terminology management systems.`"

*Register type*: vocabulary register (clause 4), with a
standards-derivative aspect: the items are normative content of
EFS 2510, clause 6, registered as items in their own right.

*Audiences*:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Audience
|Decision supported
|Format needed

|Terminology management systems
|Validate relation type identifiers in exchanged records
|TTL, JSON-LD, CSV bulk exports

|Standards authors
|Cite relation types when drafting ES documents
|Browsable site, identifiers

|SKOS consumers
|Project vocabularies using the mapping types
|TTL
|===

*Boundaries*: the register holds the relation type vocabulary; it does
not hold concept datasets, nor designation-level relations (those live
in the datasets that use the vocabulary). Adoption of concepts from
other bodies is out of scope; new relation types arrive as proposals
through EFD 101 processes.

=== Governance (clause 5)

*Control body*: the Terminology Technical Committee acting under a
charter whose essentials are:

[quote]
____
Proposals are submitted as issues in the register repository. The
Technical Committee decides by consensus recorded in the proposal
thread; where consensus is unclear, a written ballot of seven days is
opened and passes by two-thirds of votes cast excluding abstentions.
Decision records are written by the register manager within five
working days of each decision and are numbered DR-YYYY-NNN. Appeals
are addressed to the Board of Directors per EFD 101, clause 7.
____

*Roles*: register manager (runs publication runs, writes decision
records, maintains register metadata); reviewers (Terminology TC
members assigned per proposal); submitters (TC members; external
parties per EFD 101 external-member rules).

*Why governance matters here, concretely*: relation type identifiers
are consumed by validating systems. A rename would break every
consumer. The control body therefore applies the never-rename rule as
a standing decision — recorded as DR-2026-002 — which is exactly the
kind of decision a register exists to make and keep visible.

=== Planning (clause 6)

*Identifier rule*:

[cols="1,1"]
|===
|Item
|Form

|Dataset URN
|`urn:enosema:relation-vocabulary:<year>`

|Relation type URN
|`urn:enosema:relation-type:<identifier>`

|Relation type identifier
|snake_case verb or verb-pair form per EFS 2510, clause 6
|===

*Editions*: the dataset id carries the year
(`es-relation-vocabulary-2026`); a future revision with substantive
changes publishes a new edition id; item identifiers never change.

*Criteria*: the register subdivides its types by *family* (hierarchical,
equivalence, associative kinds, lexical, lifecycle, register,
versioning), recorded in the generator's canonical table and visible as
SKOS collections.

=== The dataset (clause 7)

Repository layout:

----
concept-register/
├── datasets/
│   └── es-relation-vocabulary-2026/
│       ├── register.yaml
│       ├── about-eng.md
│       └── concepts/            # 109 concept records, one file each
│           ├── broader_generic.yaml
│           ├── supersedes.yaml
│           └── …
├── site-config.yml
├── skos/relation-type.ttl       # SKOS projection (generated)
├── scripts/generate.py          # canonical table → records + SKOS
└── public/data/es-relation-vocabulary-2026/.gitkeep
----

*register.yaml* (verbatim):

[source,yaml]
----
---
schema_type: glossarist
schema_version: '3'
id: es-relation-vocabulary-2026
ref: ES Relation Vocabulary (2026)
year: 2026
urn: urn:enosema:relation-vocabulary:2026
status: current
owner: Enosema Foundation
source_repo: https://github.com/enosema/concept-register
tags:
- enosema
- relation-types
- concept-relations
- EFS-2510
- SKOS
languages:
- eng
language_order:
- eng
----

*One concept record* (`concepts/broader_generic.yaml`, verbatim):

[source,yaml]
----
---
id: broader_generic
data:
  identifier: broader_generic
  uri: urn:enosema:relation-type:broader_generic
status: valid
date_accepted: '2026-11-11T00:00:00+00:00'
sources:
- type: authoritative
  origin:
    ref:
      source: EFS 2510:2026
      clause: 6
    link: https://www.enosema.org/standards/docs/efs-2510/2026/
related:
- type: related_concept
  content:
    eng: "inverse relation type"
  ref:
    source: ES
    id: narrower_generic
---
id: broader_generic-eng
termid: broader_generic
data:
  language_code: eng
  terms:
  - type: expression
    designation: broader_generic
    normative_status: preferred
  definition:
  - content: "The current concept is a kind of the related concept (generic/specific)."
  notes:
  - content: "Inverse relation type: `narrower_generic`. Relation family: hierarchical-generic. Source: ISO 25964-1 (BTG/NTG); EFS 2510:2026, 6.2."
status: valid
----

Note the self-referential discipline: the register's own items use the
register's vocabulary (`related_concept` for the inverse-of pointer,
typed and directed), and every record cites its normative source.

*Generation*: all 109 records and the SKOS projection regenerate from
one canonical table:

[source,sh]
----
python3 scripts/generate.py
----

The generator is in the repository on purpose (EFS 2513, 10.3): the
dataset is reproducible from recorded inputs.

=== Publication (clause 7, runbook in practice)

*site-config.yml* (essentials):

[source,yaml]
----
id: enosema-concept-register
domain: www.enosema.org
uri_base: https://www.enosema.org
base_path: /concept-register
title: Enosema Concept Register
datasets:
  - id: es-relation-vocabulary-2026
    title: ES Relation Vocabulary (2026)
    local_path: datasets/es-relation-vocabulary-2026
bulk_exports: [ttl, jsonld, csv]
----

*The publication run*, as CI executes it:

. *Validate and build*: `npx @glossarist/concept-browser build` —
ingests the dataset, validates records, processes 109 concepts, builds
the site.

. *Check*: exports exist under `public/data/<dataset-id>/`; row counts
match concept counts; the TTL parses.

. *Publish*: the register build is mounted at
`https://www.enosema.org/concept-register/` by the main site pipeline,
alongside the register's own build-check workflow in its repository.

*What a failure looks like*: a definition quoting a standard without
YAML quoting (`ISO 704:2022`) breaks the parser — the run fails, the
log names the file and line, nothing publishes, and the fix is itself
a recorded change. This failure has occurred in practice; the quoting
rule of EFS 2516, 4.4 exists because of it.

=== SKOS projection (EFS 2516, clause 6)

The projection keeps what SKOS can carry and annotates the rest:

[source,turtle]
----
@prefix skos: <http://www.w3.org/2004/02/skos/core#> .
@prefix dcterms: <http://purl.org/dc/terms/> .
@prefix owl: <http://www.w3.org/2002/07/owl#> .
@prefix eno: <urn:enosema:relation-type:> .

<urn:enosema:relation-vocabulary:2026> a skos:ConceptScheme ;
    dcterms:title "ES Relation Vocabulary"@en ;
    dcterms:creator "Enosema Foundation" ;
    skos:hasTopConcept eno:broader_generic, eno:broader_partitive,
        eno:equivalent, eno:deprecates, eno:has_version .

eno:broader_generic a skos:Concept ;
    skos:prefLabel "broader_generic"@en ;
    skos:definition "The current concept is a kind of the related
        concept (generic/specific)."@en ;
    skos:inScheme <urn:enosema:relation-vocabulary:2026> ;
    owl:inverseOf eno:narrower_generic ;
    dcterms:source "ISO 25964-1 (BTG/NTG); EFS 2510:2026, 6.2" .
----

The scheme, families as collections, and per-type inverse statements
are generated; the full records remain the record of authority
(round-trip prohibition, EFS 2516, 6.3).

=== Improvement in practice (clause 12)

Two real improvement decisions from this register's short life, as
decision records would record them:

* The registry initially could not categorize the items: the concept
records carried a doctype the aggregation mapping did not know. The
fix spanned the vocabulary spec, the record format, and the registry
configuration — one improvement decision, three coordinated changes,
one verification run.

* The quoting defect (clause 7 of this annex) produced a build failure
that stopped a publication run; the sweep added the quoting rule to
the interchange spec and the lint expectation to review checklists.
Record loop and process loop, in one entry.

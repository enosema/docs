.Foreword
The Enosema Foundation ("`ES`") is the premier non-profit standardization
organization for terminology standardization and terminology related processes
such as management. It facilitates the education, standardization, research,
promotion, definition, and usage of terminology resources and management
practices globally.

ES works with international partners and experts across the globe, reflecting
the international nature of its mission. More information about ES is available
on the official website (https://www.enosema.org).

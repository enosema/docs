[[ipr]]
== Intellectual property rights

=== Free of restrictions

Text of all working documents as well as co-developed/co-published deliverables will remain free of IPR restrictions that would limit their application.

=== Copyright

Copyright of standardization deliverables created under this document
is vested in ES.

Copyright of contributions remains with their authors; contributors
grant ES the license described under <<member-sourced>> for the
development and publishing of the deliverables.


[[publication-license]]
=== Publication license

In fulfilment of the commitment that the text of ES documents remains
free of restrictions that would limit their application, ES makes its
published documents available to the public under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International Public License (CC BY 4.0).


[[member-sourced]]
=== Member-sourced documents

Authors of ES deliverables grant ES a world-wide,
non-exclusive, non-sub-licensable license
(except for the submission and adoption by other standardization bodies),
to use contents of submitted documents to ES,
for the development and publishing of the deliverables.

=== Patented content

Patented content in a standard will be governed by the ES patent policy
(<<EFD103>>) and the ES IPR policy (<<EFD105>>).

Any patents involved knowingly in an ES deliverable must be made
transparent prior to the DIS stage.

[[anatomy]]
== Anatomy of a concept entry

=== General

An entry is judged as a whole: a perfect definition in an incomplete
entry is not a good entry. This clause fixes the element inventory of a
concept entry, the obligations attached to each element, and the
mapping to the machine-readable record, so that every later clause and
criterion refers to a shared structure.

=== Element inventory

A concept entry comprises the following elements. The obligation column
gives the baseline obligation of this document; registers may promote
optional elements to mandatory in their domain profile.

[cols="1,2,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Element
|Content
|Obligation
|Primary quality dimension

|Identifier
|Stable identifier and URN (EFS 2513 identifier scheme)
|Mandatory
|Completeness (C)

|Designation(s)
|One preferred designation; admitted/deprecated forms as applicable,
each with term type and normative status
|Mandatory (preferred)
|Designation (D)

|Grammar information
|Part of speech and applicable grammar features per designation
|Conditional (mandatory where the language model requires it)
|Designation (D)

|Definition
|One definition stating the intension
|Mandatory
|Definition (N)

|Notes
|Supplementary information that does not belong in the definition
|Optional
|Clarity (K)

|Examples
|Instances illustrating the concept
|Optional
|Clarity (K), Completeness (C)

|Sources
|Origin of the concept and of the definition content
|Mandatory
|Completeness (C)

|Domain(s)
|Subject field or fields of the concept
|Mandatory (at least one)
|Consistency (S)

|Relations
|Typed relations to other concepts per EFS 2510
|Mandatory where they exist and are known
|Consistency (S)

|Lifecycle metadata
|Registration status, acceptance dates, history
|Mandatory
|Completeness (C)

|Localizations
|Localized concepts per language, with equivalence degrees
|Per register policy (EFS 2504)
|All dimensions
|===

=== Mapping to record format and to ISO practice

The entry model maps one-to-one onto the machine-readable record of
EFS 2516 (managed concept plus localized concepts), and onto the
terminological entry of ISO 10241-1 and the term entry of DIR2, clause
16.2:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|This document
|EFS 2516 record
|DIR2 / ISO 10241-1

|identifier + lifecycle metadata
|managed concept `data`
|entry number, entry status

|designation(s) + grammar
|localized concept `terms`
|term entries (16.4)

|definition
|localized concept `definition`
|definition (16.5.1)

|notes / examples
|localized concept `notes` / `examples`
|notes (16.5.5) / examples (16.5.6)

|sources
|`sources`
|source citation (16.7)

|domain(s)
|dataset assignment / tags
|subject field

|relations
|`related` + n-ary relation files
|`(not in DIR2; EFS 2510)`
|===

A reviewer reads one structure whichever representation is in front of
them; the scoring criteria of clause <<scoring>> cite the elements of
this clause by name.

=== The unit of judgement

The unit of judgement is the *entry*, not the element in isolation:
criteria in the dimension groups address elements, but the entry score
and grade (<<scoring>>) belong to the entry. This is deliberate — the
corpus shows that defects cluster (an entry with an empty definition is
also likely to lack notes and examples), and a per-element model would
hide the compound failure that review needs to catch.

=== Record mapping — verbatim example

The anatomy in record form, for a minimal conforming entry (EFS 2516
format; managed concept document followed by its localized concept
document):

[source,yaml]
----
---
id: ship-water-draught
data:
  identifier: ship-water-draught
  uri: urn:example:ship:water-draught
  localized_concepts:
    eng: water-draught-eng
status: valid
date_accepted: '2026-01-15T00:00:00+00:00'
---
id: water-draught-eng
termid: ship-water-draught
data:
  language_code: eng
  terms:
  - type: expression
    designation: water draught
    normative_status: preferred
    grammar_info: [{noun: true}]
  definition:
  - content: vertical distance between the waterline and the lowest
    continuous point of the hull of a ship
  examples:
  - content: a vessel drawing 12 m cannot enter the inner harbour
  sources:
  - origin:
      ref: {source: ISO, id: ISO 19642, clause: 3.2.1}
      type: authoritative
status: valid
----

Every anatomical element has a home in the record: identifier and
lifecycle metadata in the managed document; designation, grammar,
definition, examples and sources in the localized document; relations
on the managed document; domains as dataset assignment. A reviewer
armed with this mapping can score any conforming record without a
mapping table.

=== Element obligations and profiles

Registers declare a *profile* that adjusts obligations: a pedagogical
register promotes examples to mandatory (C-3); a legal register
promotes regional-variation notes; a machine-consumed vocabulary
register demotes examples entirely. Profiles shall not demote
mandatory elements of <<anatomy>> — the floor is the floor — but they
may promote, and the promotion is itself a recorded profile decision
(EFS 2513, 10.3).

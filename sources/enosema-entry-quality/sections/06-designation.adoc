[[designation-quality]]
== Designation quality (Group D)

=== General

A concept can carry several designations, but one is *preferred*: the
form that consumers should use and that definitions, publications and
indexes lead with. Designation quality is about choosing that form
well, annotating it fully, and being honest about admitted and
deprecated alternatives.

=== Rules

D-R1:: The preferred designation shall denote exactly the concept the
entry defines: reviewing the designation alone, a domain expert should
not expect a different intension (ISO 704).

D-R2:: The preferred designation shall follow the morphological and
orthographic conventions of the entry language, including singular
form for countable nouns (DIR2, 16.4.4), established order of
modifiers, and current spelling.

D-R3:: Term type shall be recorded per ISO 12620 (expression,
abbreviation, short form, initialism, symbol, formula, graphical) and
normative status shall be recorded (preferred, admitted, deprecated)
per DIR2, 16.4.1.

D-R4:: Grammar information shall be recorded for the designation:
part of speech, and gender/number/plural forms where the language
model of the register requires them. In the corpus, 98,9 % of entries
carry grammar information; a conforming termbase matches this (EFS
2516 record, `grammar_info`).

D-R5:: An abbreviation may be preferred only where it is at least as
understood in the domain as the full form, and the full form shall be
recorded as an admitted designation of the same concept.

D-R6:: A deprecated designation shall remain in the entry with its
status, never deleted, and where a replacement exists the entry or
relation set shall state it (EFS 2510 lifecycle relations).

D-R7:: Designations shall not contain requirements language,
quantifiers, or document references; they designate, they do not
prescribe.

D-R8:: Designations within one termbase shall be consistent in form
for coordinate concepts: a register that expresses one criterion of
subdivision as "`… by mass`" should not express its coordinate as
"`… according to power`" (see Group S).

=== Scoring criteria

The designation criteria scored in <<scoring>>:

dc1:: Preferred designation aptness (D-1, D-2) — does the form denote
the concept, at the right conceptual level?

dc2:: Grammatical correctness (D-3).

dc3:: Annotation completeness (D-4): term type, status, grammar
present for all designations.

dc4:: Form discipline (D-5, D-7): abbreviation handling, no
requirements language, singular discipline.

[example]
Working example — a good designation set for a concept in a shipping
register:

[source,yaml]
----
terms:
- type: expression
  designation: air draught
  normative_status: preferred
  grammar_info: [{noun: true}]
- type: abbreviation
  designation: air draught  # marked as full form of the abbreviation below
  normative_status: admitted
- type: abbreviation
  designation: AD
  normative_status: admitted
----

The full form is admitted while the abbreviation carries usage; both
are recorded; the deprecated case (for example a pre-standardization
"`air height`") would appear with `normative_status: deprecated` and a
pointer to the preferred form, never silently removed.

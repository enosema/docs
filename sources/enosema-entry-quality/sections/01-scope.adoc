== Scope

This document specifies quality for terminology concept entries: what a
good designation is, what a good definition is, what a complete entry
contains, what clarity requires, and what consistency across a termbase
demands. It specifies grouped requirements with stable identifiers, a
scoring model that measures entry quality against those requirements,
and checklists for term convenors and reviewers.

It applies to:

* concept entries in ES terminology registers and termbases, in the
record format of EFS 2516;

* entries drafted for standards and other publications under the ES
Directives; and

* any termbase whose operator adopts this document.

This document does not specify:

* the management system around the entries (EFS 2513);

* the semantics or enumeration of concept relations (EFS 2510);

* the wire format of interchange (EFS 2516);

* how to operate a register (EFG 2519); or

* domain-specific terminology conventions, which domain registers
declare as profiles on top of this document.

== Term entry reviewer checklist

The checklist walks the reviewer through one entry in order. Each line
names the criterion it feeds and the requirements it examines. Lines
marked ⛔ correspond to mandatory-fix flags (<<mandatory-fix-flags>>).

=== 0. Gate

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|⛔
|Is the definition present and non-empty?
|cc1, flag

|⛔
|Are identifier, preferred designation, domain(s), sources, and
lifecycle metadata present?
|cc1, flag
|===

=== 1. Designation

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|
|Exactly one preferred designation?
|dc1 (D-1)

|
|Would a domain expert read the preferred designation as this concept
(and not a neighbour)?
|dc1 (D-2)

|
|Grammatically correct; singular for countable nouns; spelling per
convention?
|dc2 (D-3)

|
|Term type recorded (ISO 12620)?
|dc3 (D-4)

|
|Normative status recorded?
|dc3 (D-4)

|
|Grammar information recorded?
|dc3 (D-4)

|
|Abbreviations: full form admitted alongside; symbol types declared?
|dc4 (D-5)

|
|No requirements language, quantifiers or document references in the
designation?
|dc4 (D-7)

|
|Deprecated forms retained with pointers?
|dc4 (D-6)
|===

=== 2. Definition

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|
|Exactly one definition (per language)?
|nc1 (N-1)

|
|Genus first?
|nc1 (N-2)

|
|Delimiting characteristics present — distinguishes the concept from
its coordinate concepts?
|nc1 (N-2, N-7)

|
|Substitution test passes (apply it in a defining context)?
|nc2 (N-3) ⛔ on outright failure

|
|No circularity (designation or derivative used as defining element)?
|nc3 (N-4)

|
|No shall/should/must/need?
|nc3 (N-5) ⛔

|
|No article at the head?
|nc3 (N-6)

|
|Single declarative sentence?
|nc3 (N-6)

|
|Self-contained within the register?
|nc1 (N-7)

|
|Word count in the working band (corpus median 16, p90 32) — beyond
it, is content displaced to notes?
|nc4 (N-8)

|
|Notes carry only non-delimiting content?
|nc4 (N-8, N-R10)

|
|Localizations faithful, degrees and notes recorded?
|nc5 (N-9)
|===

=== 3. Completeness

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|
|Sources present?
|cc2 (C-2)

|
|Sources precise (document, clause, resolvable link)?
|cc2 (C-2)

|
|Examples present where profile/warranted?
|cc3 (C-3)

|
|Notes present where misunderstanding is foreseeable?
|cc3 (C-4)

|
|Domains present, from the domain vocabulary?
|cc1 (C-6), sc3 (S-3)

|
|Declared localizations present with degrees and notes?
|cc4 (C-5)

|
|Known relations and mappings recorded?
|cc5 (C-6), sc2 (S-2)
|===

=== 4. Clarity

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|
|Can a competent outsider apply the entry without drafting history?
|kc1 (K-1)

|
|One reading per sentence?
|kc2 (K-1)

|
|Units/reference frame stated or unambiguous?
|kc2 (K-2)

|
|Abbreviations expanded at first use?
|kc3 (K-3)

|
|References precise (identifier / document + clause)?
|kc3 (K-4)

|
|Language correctness and register per convention?
|kc4 (K-5)

|
|Notes carry context, not argument?
|kc4 (K-6)

|
|Examples concrete, correct, consistent?
|kc4 (K-7)
|===

=== 5. Consistency

[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|✓|Question
|Feeds

|
|Coordinate concepts in parallel form?
|sc1 (S-1)

|
|Genus named by the same term across the coordinate set?
|sc1 (S-2)

|
|Relations exist in vocabulary, direction correct?
|sc2 (S-2)

|
|Hierarchy acyclic from this entry?
|sc2 (S-2, ac3)

|
|Coordinates delimited (MECE)?
|sc2 (ac5)

|
|Domain tags valid?
|sc3 (S-3)

|
|Mappings honest and named?
|sc4 (S-4)

|
|Word-building consistent with the terminological family?
|sc5 (S-5)

|
|Status coherent with coordinate set?
|sc5 (S-6)
|===

=== 6. Record

Score the criteria, check flags, compute the entry score and grade
(<<scoring>>), write findings per failed criterion quoting the entry's
own words, and record the score sheet (clause <<review-process>>).

[[consistency]]
== Consistency (Group S)

=== General

Consistency is the termbase-level dimension: an entry can be internally
excellent and still clash with its neighbours. Group S requirements are
evaluated against the termbase, not the entry alone, and they are the
dimension that term convenors own most directly.

=== Rules

S-R1:: *Coordinate parallelism.* Coordinate concepts (same parent,
same criterion of subdivision) are expressed in parallel grammatical
form and use parallel designation patterns; a coordinate set that
mixes patterns signals either a wrong placement or a missing
criterion (ISO 704 concept-system discipline; EFS 2510, clause 4).

S-R2:: *Definition harmony.* Definitions of coordinate concepts share
the genus formulation: the same superordinate concept is named by the
same term, and delimiting characteristics are stated in comparable
terms. Divergent genus naming within one coordinate set is a defect.

S-R3:: *Relation correctness.* The entry's relations use the
vocabulary of EFS 2510 with correct direction, consistent with the
entry's placement; hierarchical relations are acyclic (EFS 2510
constraint ac3) and coordinate sets satisfy the delimiting
characteristic rule (EFS 2510 ac5).

S-R4:: *Domain tagging discipline.* The domain(s) of the entry use the
register's domain vocabulary; a concept tagged outside its domain or
tagged with a non-existent domain breaks dataset organization and
search.

S-R5:: *Cross-register mapping honesty.* Mappings to other
vocabularies use the mapping relation types and name the external
vocabulary (EFS 2510); an `exact_match` asserted where only
`close_match` holds corrupts downstream alignment.

S-R6:: *Terminological family harmony.* Related entries of a family
(derivations, partitives, processes and their products) use consistent
word-building and parallel definitions, so that the family reads as a
system (ISO 704).

S-R7:: *Status coherence.* Where one concept in a coordinate set is
superseded or deprecated, the reviewer checks whether the whole set's
status is coherent with the system's evolution; a mixed-status
coordinate set with unchanged criterion is a warning.

=== Scoring criteria

The consistency criteria scored in <<scoring>>:

sc1:: Coordinate parallelism and genus harmony (S-1).

sc2:: Relation correctness (S-2).

sc3:: Domain discipline (S-3).

sc4:: Mapping honesty (S-4).

sc5:: Family harmony and status coherence (S-5, S-6).

[example]
Coordinate parallelism — a conforming set and a defective set:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Conforming (parallel)
|Defective (divergent)

|*water draught*: vertical distance between the waterline and the
lowest point of a ship

*air draught*: vertical distance between the waterline and the highest
point of a ship

*length overall*: distance between the foremost and aftermost points
of a ship
|*water draught*: vertical distance between the waterline and the
lowest point of a ship

*air draught*: the height of the ship above the waterline
|===

The defective set breaks genus harmony — "`distance between …`"
versus "`the height of …`" — and states the air draught by
delimitation against the wrong reference; a reviewer flags S-1 and
proposes the parallel form.

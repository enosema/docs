[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions
apply.

[[term-entry]]
==== concept entry
alt:[term entry]

structured record of one concept, comprising its designations,
definition, notes, examples, sources, relations, and management
metadata

[SOURCE: ISO 10241-1, modified — extended to machine-readable records]

[[term-designation]]
==== designation

representation of a concept by a sign which denotes it

[NOTE: In this document, designations are classified per ISO 12620:
expression, abbreviation, short form, initialism, symbol, formula,
graphical, and their variants.]

[[term-definition]]
==== definition

representation of a concept by a descriptive statement which serves to
differentiate it from related concepts

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-substitution-test]]
==== substitution test

check that a definition can replace the term in defining context
without loss of meaning or grammaticality

[[term-genus]]
==== genus

superordinate concept whose intension is presupposed by a definition
and whose delimiting characteristics the definition supplies

[[term-essential-characteristic]]
==== essential characteristic

characteristic of a concept that is required by the concept's
intension

[[term-delimiting-characteristic]]
==== delimiting characteristic

essential characteristic that distinguishes a concept from its
coordinate concepts

[[term-entry-score]]
==== entry score

weighted aggregate of the criterion scores of a _concept entry_
(<<term-entry>>), expressed on a scale of 0 to 100

[[term-grade]]
==== grade

band to which an _entry score_ (<<term-entry-score>>) belongs,
denoted A, B, C or D

[[term-requirement-group]]
==== requirement group

named set of requirements of this document bearing a common stable
identifier prefix: D (designation), N (defiNition), C (completeness),
K (clarity), S (consistency)

[[term-convenor]]
==== term convenor

reviewer responsible for the terminology quality of a register or
document, applying the checklists and scoring model of this document

[[term-anti-pattern]]
==== anti-pattern

recurring defect pattern observed in the corpus of clause <<corpus>>,
catalogued with its detection rule and fix in Annex <<anti-patterns>>

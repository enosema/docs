[[anti-patterns]]
[appendix]
== Anti-pattern catalogue

Each anti-pattern: the recurring defect observed in the corpus of
clause <<corpus>>, its detection rule (how a reviewer or validator
finds it), the requirement it violates, and the fix.

=== AP-01 — The silent entry

*Defect*: an entry with an empty or missing definition (corpus: 1,2 %
of entries; concentrated in 1983–2012 legacy documents).
*Detection*: absence of a non-empty `definition` block — fully
mechanical.
*Violates*: C-1 (flag).
*Fix*: draft the definition from the concept's system placement; if
the concept cannot be defined, it should not be an entry.

=== AP-02 — The buried rake

*Defect*: a definition that enumerates a part list ("`a system
consisting of a sensor, a processor, a display and a power unit`").
*Detection*: definitions containing enumerations (", and a", ",
consisting of").
*Violates*: N-8, N-R10.
*Fix*: express the joint decomposition as an n-ary relation per
EFS 2510, clause 7; keep the definition on the intension.

=== AP-03 — The mirror definition

*Defect*: definitional circularity — the designation or its derivative
used as the defining element (corpus heuristic: 3,5 %, part of which
is legitimate term-mention).
*Detection*: designation string (or stem) appears in the definition —
mechanical flag, human judgement on each hit.
*Violates*: N-4.
*Fix*: ground the genus outside the term; if the concept truly has no
superordinate, define by enumeration of essential characteristics
(ISO 704 extensional definition).

=== AP-04 — The synonym swap

*Defect*: the definition defines a *different* concept that shares the
designation in another domain or register.
*Detection*: cross-register mapping review; substitution test failure
in the domain context.
*Violates*: D-2, S-4.
*Fix*: separate concepts with mapping relations (`related_match` until
a degree is assessed), or harmonize the concept with its counterpart
per EFS 2513 change procedures.

=== AP-05 — The smuggled requirement

*Defect*: requirements language inside the definition (corpus: 0,5 %).
*Detection*: shall/should/must/need in the definition content —
mechanical.
*Violates*: N-5 (flag).
*Fix*: move the prescription into the normative document text where it
belongs; the definition describes the concept as it is.

=== AP-06 — The orphan abbreviation

*Defect*: an abbreviation designated as preferred with no full form in
the entry.
*Detection*: preferred designation of type abbreviation without an
admitted full-form designation.
*Violates*: D-5.
*Fix*: record the full form as admitted (or the abbreviation as
admitted and the full form as preferred, per domain usage).

=== AP-07 — The article opener

*Defect*: definitions beginning with "`A`", "`An`" or "`The`" (corpus:
3,1 %).
*Detection*: first token of the definition in {A, An, The} —
mechanical.
*Violates*: N-6.
*Fix*: delete the article; the definition begins with the genus.

=== AP-08 — The unbounded definition

*Defect*: definitions far beyond the working band (corpus p90: 32
words; defects observed to 159 words), usually enumerations, process
descriptions, or requirements displaced into the definition.
*Detection*: definition word count beyond the register's stated band.
*Violates*: N-8, N-R8.
*Fix*: split per AP-02, or displace mechanism detail into notes; find
the missing intermediate concept.

=== AP-09 — The vague pointer

*Defect*: references from definitions or notes to "`the relevant
standard`", "`see above`", "`applicable regulations`".
*Detection*: pointer phrases in definition/notes — near-mechanical.
*Violates*: K-4.
*Fix*: cite by identifier or document + clause.

=== AP-10 — The example that teaches the wrong concept

*Defect*: an example whose content matches a neighbouring concept
better than the entry's concept.
*Detection*: examples checked against the definition at review (K-7);
examples that contradict the definition or are identical to a
coordinate concept's example.
*Violates*: K-7.
*Fix*: correct the instance, or accept that the entry boundary is
wrong and address the concept system (AP-02's cousin).

=== AP-11 — The status-blind import

*Defect*: entries imported from legacy corpora carrying their
conversion-era defects silently (empty definitions, missing examples,
unvalidated designations).
*Detection*: import scoring per clause <<review-process>>; document-era
correlation (clause <<corpus>>).
*Violates*: C-1, C-2, EFG 2519 import review.
*Fix*: re-validate on import; score before acceptance; remediate per
EFG 2519 clause 12.

=== AP-12 — The family stranger

*Defect*: a member of a terminological family (derivations, partitives)
whose word-building or definition structure diverges from the family
pattern.
*Detection*: family-level review (Group S); word-formation comparison
across the family.
*Violates*: S-5.
*Fix*: align word-building and definition structure with the family,
or determine that the divergent member is a different family's concept.

[[clarity]]
== Clarity and language (Group K)

=== General

Clarity is the reader-facing dimension: whether a competent outsider —
a translator, an engineer from another field, a new committee member —
can understand and correctly apply the entry without access to its
drafting history. Clarity failures are rarely rule-violations; they are
communication failures, which is why this group's requirements are
stated as obligations on the *reader's experience*.

=== Rules

K-R1:: *Plain domain language.* The definition and notes use the
ordinary technical language of the domain; constructions that only a
drafting insider can parse (nested noun stacks, undefined shorthand)
are defects.

K-R2:: *One reading.* Each sentence of the definition and notes has
exactly one reasonable reading. Pronouns without clear antecedents,
ambiguous attachment ("`system for ships and ports that operate`"),
and equivocal quantifiers are defects.

K-R3:: *Units are stated.* Where the concept involves measurable
extent, the applicable unit system or reference frame is stated or
unambiguous from the domain (per the applicable ISO 80000 part).

K-R4:: *Abbreviations are expanded at first use.* Any abbreviation
used inside the definition, notes or examples is expanded at its first
use within the entry, unless it is more standard in the domain than
its expansion.

K-R5:: *Reference discipline.* Concepts referenced from the definition
or notes are cited by their identifiers or by document + clause; vague
pointers ("`see above`", "`the relevant standard`") are defects.

K-R6:: *Language correctness.* The entry language is used correctly:
spelling per the register's language convention, agreement, and
terminological register consistent with the register's stated audience
(register of the designations per ISO 12620 data categories).

K-R7:: *Notes carry context, not argument.* Notes explain scope,
boundaries, regional differences, and points of caution; they do not
argue the case for the definition or narrate its drafting history.

K-R8:: *Examples are concrete and correct.* An example is a real or
realistic instance of the concept, checkable against the definition;
an example that contradicts the definition or extends it into a
different concept is a defect.

=== Scoring criteria

The clarity criteria scored in <<scoring>>:

kc1:: Outsiderness (K-1): can a competent outsider apply the entry
without the drafting history?

kc2:: Ambiguity absence (K-1, K-2): one reading, units resolved.

kc3:: Reference discipline (K-3, K-4).

kc4:: Note and example quality (K-6, K-7).

[example]
Clarity revision — the same content before and after:

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
|Unclear
|Clear

|system for the remote monitoring and control of ships and port
installations based on a standardized protocol framework and relevant
standards
|system for the remote monitoring and control of ships and of port
installations, operating over the protocol framework defined in
ISO 20141
|===

The unclear form stacks nouns, attaches "`based on`" ambiguously, and
points at "`relevant standards`". The clear form names each controlled
object, cites the protocol framework precisely (K-4), and reads one
way (K-1).

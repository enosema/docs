[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

ISO and ES maintain terminological databases for use in standardization at the
addresses given in the bibliography.

[[term-concept]]
==== concept

unit of knowledge created by a unique combination of characteristics

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

NOTE: A concept is not necessarily bound to a particular language. It is,
however, influenced by the social or cultural background which often leads to
different categorizations.

[[term-concept-system]]
==== concept system

set of concepts and the set of relations that hold among them

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1]

[[term-concept-relation]]
==== concept relation

semantic relation between two or more concepts within a concept system

[NOTE: In this document, a relation between exactly two concepts is a
*binary relation* (<<term-binary-relation>>); a relation between a
comprehensive concept and two or more coordinate concepts is an
*n-ary relation* (<<term-nary-relation>>).]

[[term-binary-relation]]
==== binary relation

_concept relation_ (<<term-concept-relation>>) that holds between exactly two
concepts

[[term-nary-relation]]
==== n-ary relation
alt:[hyperedge]
_concept relation_ (<<term-concept-relation>>) that holds between a
comprehensive concept and two or more coordinate concepts that are jointly
significant with respect to the comprehensive concept

[[term-coordinate-concept]]
==== coordinate concept

concept to which a given _concept_ (<<term-concept>>) is related by a shared
superordinate concept and a shared criterion of subdivision

[SOURCE: ISO 1087-1, modified — the shared criterion of subdivision is made
explicit]

[[term-comprehensive-concept]]
==== comprehensive concept

superordinate concept that subsumes two or more subordinate concepts which,
taken together, characterize it

[NOTE: In a partitive relation, the comprehensive concept is the whole that
the parts fitted together constitute; in a generic relation, it is the class
that the kinds jointly constitute; in a sequential relation, it is the process
or development that the ordered stages jointly constitute.]

[[term-criterion-of-subdivision]]
==== criterion of subdivision
alt:[dimension]

basis for the division of a concept system into subordinate concepts

[SOURCE: ISO 704]

[[term-multidimensionality]]
==== multidimensionality

characteristic of a _concept system_ (<<term-concept-system>>) in which the
concepts of one subdivision level are subdivided according to more than one
_criterion of subdivision_ (<<term-criterion-of-subdivision>>)

[SOURCE: ISO 704]

[[term-completeness]]
==== completeness

indication of whether the members of an _n-ary relation_
(<<term-nary-relation>>) exhaust the intension of the comprehensive concept

[[term-presence]]
==== presence

indication of whether a member of an _n-ary relation_
(<<term-nary-relation>>) exists in every instance of the comprehensive
concept or only in some instances

[[term-delimiting-characteristic]]
==== delimiting characteristic

_characteristic_ of a concept that distinguishes it from coordinate concepts
within the same subdivision

[NOTE: In concept diagrams, the part of a partitive relation that delimits a
subordinate concept is shown with a bold line of three-fold width.]

[[term-sequential-relation]]
==== sequential relation

_n-ary relation_ (<<term-nary-relation>>) connecting a comprehensive concept
to two or more subordinate concepts ordered by a criterion of sequencing

[[term-register]]
==== register

set of information items with an assigned status maintained by a
registration authority

[SOURCE: ISO 19135-1]

[[term-registration-authority]]
==== registration authority
alt:[RA]

organization that performs the administrative functions required to operate a
_register_ (<<term-register>>)

[SOURCE: ISO 19135-1]

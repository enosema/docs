[[representation]]
== Machine-readable representation

=== General

Concept relations shall be represented in machine-readable form so that
concept systems can be validated, interchanged, and published. This
clause specifies the representation requirements; it does not mandate a
serialization format, but specifies the semantic content that any
conforming serialization shall carry and the vocabulary mappings that
interchange formats shall use.

=== Semantic content of a relation record

A conforming relation record carries, for a binary relation:

. the relation type identifier (<<rel-vocabulary>>);

. the identifier of the current (subject) concept;

. the identifier of the related (object) concept;

. optionally, an explanatory content string;

. optionally, references to concept sources (bibliographic support); and

. optionally, the registration status of the relation assertion itself.

For an n-ary relation, a conforming record additionally carries the kind
of the relation, the comprehensive concept identifier, the ordered member
list with per-member presence, count and delimiting metadata, the
completeness, and the criterion (<<nary-relations>>).

[[repr-skos]]
=== Mapping to SKOS

Where a concept system is published as a SKOS concept scheme, the
following mappings shall be used:

[cols="1,1",options="header"]
|===
|ES relation type
|SKOS property

|`broader` / `broader_generic` / `broader_partitive` /
`broader_instantial`
|`skos:broader`

|`narrower` / `narrower_generic` / `narrower_partitive` /
`narrower_instantial`
|`skos:narrower`

|`related_concept` and associative types
|`skos:related`

|`exact_match`
|`skos:exactMatch`

|`close_match`
|`skos:closeMatch`

|`broad_match`
|`skos:broadMatch`

|`narrow_match`
|`skos:narrowMatch`

|`related_match`
|`skos:relatedMatch`
|===

The projection discards ES-specific information: coordination method,
inverse pairing, per-member presence and count, delimiting status,
completeness, and criterion are not carried by SKOS. Publishers shall
retain the full ES record and treat the SKOS projection as lossy.
Systems shall not round-trip ES data through SKOS alone.

[[repr-jsonld]]
=== JSON-LD and typed representation

Where concept systems are published as JSON-LD or as typed records:

* relation types shall be identified by the identifiers of
<<rel-vocabulary>>, resolvable through the ES relation register
(<<rel-register>>);

* inverses may be materialized but shall remain consistent with the
recorded direction;

* n-ary relations shall be represented as first-class resources, not as
reified pairs, with members referenced by identifier and per-member
metadata attached to the membership, not to the concept.

=== Relation identifiers in interchange

Relation type identifiers used in interchange shall be exactly the
identifiers of <<rel-vocabulary>> or of a domain profile registered per
<<rel-register>>. Systems shall reject interchange containing unknown
relation type identifiers rather than silently dropping the relations,
and shall report them to the sender.

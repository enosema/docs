== Scope

This document specifies the concept systems and concept relations used in
terminology management, for the purposes of the Enosema Foundation and of
any organization that adopts this document.

It specifies:

* the structure of concept systems, including concept coordination
(generic, partitive and instantial), criteria of subdivision, and
multidimensionality;

* a controlled vocabulary of concept relations, enumerating each relation
type with its semantics, direction, and inverse;

* the rules for choosing between binary relations and n-ary relations
(hyperedges), and the metadata that n-ary relations carry, including
completeness, per-member presence and count, delimiting status, and the
criterion of sequencing;

* requirements for the machine-readable representation of concept
relations and for their management within registers.

This document does not specify:

* the structure of terminological entries in standards, which is
specified in ISO 10241-1 and applied in EFS 2501;

* the management of multilingual equivalence beyond its impact on
relations, which is specified in EFS 2504;

* the abstract data model of concept relationships, which is specified
in EFS 2507;

* the organizational requirements for operating a concept management
system, which are specified in EFS 2513.

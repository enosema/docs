[[rel-vocabulary]]
== Concept relation vocabulary

=== General

The ES relation vocabulary is a controlled vocabulary. Each relation type
is defined by:

* its *identifier*, used in data interchange and in registers;

* its *semantics*, stated as the condition under which the relation holds;

* its *direction*, stated in the form "`the current concept … the related
concept`"; and

* its *inverse*, the relation type that holds when the direction is
reversed.

Relation types of this vocabulary shall be referenced by their identifiers.
Concept management systems shall use the identifiers exactly as given in
this clause; systems that require additional relation types shall propose
them for registration in the ES relation register (<<rel-register>>) and
shall not extend the vocabulary locally.

Every directed relation type has an inverse in the vocabulary. Where an
assertion is recorded, systems should record the inverse explicitly or
make it derivable without ambiguity. Symmetric relation types
(``opposite_of``, ``equivalent``, ``exact_match``, ``related_match``,
``compare``, ``related_concept``) have no distinct inverse.

[[rel-hierarchical]]
=== Hierarchical relations

Hierarchical relations express membership of a subordinate concept in a
superordinate concept. The vocabulary distinguishes the three coordination
methods of <<concept-systems>>.

.Table — Generic relation types
[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|Identifier
|Semantics
|Inverse

|`broader_generic`
|The current concept is a kind of the related concept.
|`narrower_generic`

|`narrower_generic`
|The related concept is a kind of the current concept.
|`broader_generic`

|`broader`
|The current concept is subsumed by the related concept, coordination
method not stated.
|`narrower`

|`narrower`
|The related concept is subsumed by the current concept, coordination
method not stated.
|`broader`
|===

NOTE: The unqualified `broader`/`narrower` pair is provided for
interchange with SKOS. Within ES concept systems, the coordination method
shall be stated whenever it is known; the unqualified pair is a fallback,
not a substitute.

[[rel-equivalence]]
=== Equivalence and mapping relations

Equivalence relations assert correspondence between concepts. Mapping
relations assert correspondence between concepts of *different
vocabularies*, in the sense of ISO 25964-2 and SKOS.

.Table — Equivalence and mapping relation types
[cols="1,2,1",options="header"]
|===
|Identifier
|Semantics
|Inverse

|`equivalent`
|The current concept is equivalent in denotation to the related concept.
|`equivalent` (symmetric)

|`exact_match`
|The current concept is an exact cross-vocabulary match of the related
concept, which can be replaced one-for-one.
|`exact_match` (symmetric)

|`close_match`
|The current concept is approximately equivalent to the related concept.
|`close_match` (symmetric)

|`broad_match`
|The related concept of another vocabulary is broader than the current
concept.
|`narrow_match`

|`narrow_match`
|The related concept of another vocabulary is narrower than the current
concept.
|`broad_match`

|`related_match`
|The current concept is associatively related to a concept of another
vocabulary.
|`related_match` (symmetric)

|`compare`
|The current concept may be compared to the related concept to clarify
distinctions.
|`compare` (symmetric)

|`contrast`
|The current concept is understood in contrast to the related concept.
|`contrast` (symmetric)
|===

NOTE: Cross-language equivalence between localized concepts is governed by
EFS 2504 and is recorded as an equivalence degree on the localization, not
as a concept relation.

[[rel-register]]
=== The relation vocabulary as a registered item

The relation vocabulary of this clause is itself a collection of managed
items. ES maintains it in a register operated under the FERIN framework
(ISO 19135 procedures), where each relation type is a registered item with
a stable identifier, a definition, a registration status, and a version
history.

A relation type proposed for use in ES concept systems that is not in this
vocabulary shall be submitted to the register as a proposal. Accepted
proposals are added to the vocabulary in a new version of this document.
Systems shall not use unregistered relation type identifiers in
interchange.

INFO: The register is publicly available at the Enosema Standards Registry
(https://www.enosema.org/standards/).

[[quality]]
== Quality constraints on concept systems

=== General

A concept system degrades through use: concepts are added without their
relations, decompositions are edited without their criteria, and
lifecycle events are recorded without their counterparts. This clause
specifies the constraints that concept management systems shall enforce
to keep a concept system coherent. The constraints are stated so that
they are mechanically checkable.

=== Structural constraints

The following constraints shall hold for every concept system.

ac1:: Every relation type identifier in the system belongs to the
vocabulary of <<rel-vocabulary>> or to a registered domain profile
(<<rel-register>>).

ac2:: Every binary relation references concepts that exist in the
system; dangling references are not permitted.

ac3:: Every hierarchical relation is acyclic: a concept shall not be its
own ancestor or descendant through any chain of hierarchical relations.

ac4:: Every subdivision in the system states its criterion of
subdivision; every n-ary relation states its criterion of subdivision or
sequencing.

ac5:: Every subordinate concept within one subdivision is delimited from
its coordinate concepts by at least one delimiting characteristic
(<<concept-systems>>).

ac6:: Where two parallel subdivisions of the same concept exist, they
differ in criterion and are recorded as parallel subdivisions
(<<criteria-multidim-systems>>).

=== Constraint checking on lifecycle relations

The lifecycle relation types of <<rel-other>> are directional in time. The
following constraints apply.

lc1:: A concept cannot both `supersede` and `superseded_by` the same
target; the inverse, if materialized, shall be consistent with the
direction of the assertion.

lc2:: A retired concept shall not be the subject of new structural
relations, except relations that record the retirement itself.

lc3:: Where a concept is replaced in a register, the `replaces`/`replaced_by`
pair shall link the register items, and any `supersedes`/`superseded_by`
pair shall link the concepts themselves; systems shall not conflate the
two pairs.

=== Constraint checking on n-ary relations

na1:: Every n-ary relation has at least two members.

na2:: Every member of an n-ary relation is coordinate with the other
members: they share the comprehensive concept and the criterion.

na3:: The presence/count combination of each member is consistent
(<<nary-partitive>>): `optional` members shall not carry `exactly_one`.

na4:: A `complete` n-ary relation whose members are all `optional` is
inconsistent: a decomposition of optional members cannot exhaust the
intension of the comprehensive concept; systems shall reject the record
or require the criterion to be revised.

na5:: At most one member of an n-ary relation is marked delimiting.

=== Consistency of inverses

For every materialized inverse pair, the system shall verify that the
inverse assertion exists and uses the inverse type of <<rel-vocabulary>>.
Where a system does not materialize inverses, it shall derive them on
request from the recorded direction, and the derivation shall use the
inverse table of <<rel-vocabulary>>.

=== Review of concept systems

Concept systems shall be reviewed on a periodic basis (EFS 2513 specifies
the organizational review requirements). The review shall, at minimum:

* check the constraints of this clause against the live system;

* report subdivisions whose criterion has been lost or left implicit;

* report lifecycle events recorded without their register counterpart; and

* report SKOS projections that have diverged from the full ES record.

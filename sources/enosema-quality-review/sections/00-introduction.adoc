== Introduction

=== Why a quality review

The ES Terminology series rests on a claim: that the requirements in
its normative documents describe what good vocabulary practice is,
not what one organization happens to prefer. A claim of that kind
needs evidence. The strongest evidence available is the vocabulary
that the international system itself produces — the hundreds of
thousands of concept entries that ISO has published through its
Online Browsing Platform and the tens of thousands of concepts that
IEC TC 1 maintains as the International Electrotechnical Vocabulary.
If the requirements of the ES series are well calibrated, a corpus
conforming termbase measures well against the corpus; if the corpus
systematically fails somewhere, the requirements are set to address that
failure.

This report is the evidence base made public. It analyses the two
corpora with a fixed method (clause <<methodology>>), reports the
statistics (clauses <<iso-findings>> and <<iev-findings>>), compares
the two systems of maintenance (clause <<comparison>>), counts the
defects that the ES series calls anti-patterns (clause
<<anti-patterns>>), and states the implications of each finding for
the normative documents of the series (clause <<implications>>).

=== What the corpora are

The two corpora represent two eras and two architectures of
international vocabulary maintenance:

* The *ISO OBP corpus* is the aggregate of terminology from hundreds
of ISO documents, extracted from the published platform into
Glossarist-form records. Its entries were authored across four
decades, under successive editions of the ISO/IEC Directives and of
ISO 10241, by hundreds of committees. It measures the system as a
whole — including its oldest practices.

* The *IEV corpus* is one vocabulary, maintained continuously since
the early twentieth century and converted in its current phase into
structured records by IEC TC 1. Every concept carries a multilingual
entry set; English and French carry the authoritative definitions and
the other languages carry designations. It measures the
depth-maintenance model: one authoritative body, one vocabulary,
many languages.

The two corpora differ in shape, and the differences are findings.
Where they agree, the agreement is the strongest signal this study
can produce: two independent systems, two architectures, one
behaviour.

=== How to read this report

Each clause states its findings first and interprets them second.
Statistics appear in the body where they carry an argument and in
Annex A (ISO) and Annex B (IEV) in full. Clause <<detection-rules>>
states the operational definition of every measure — the exact rule
by which each count was made — because a defect rate is only as
honest as its detector. Clause <<reproduction>> gives the commands
and seeds.

This report makes no recommendations beyond clause <<implications>>;
it is a study, not a guideline. The documents that convert its
findings into requirements are cited throughout, and each finding
carries the traceability note that states which requirements it
informs.

=== Relation to the ES series

The report serves three documents directly:

* *EFS 2522* (concept entry quality): its criteria, weights and
anchors were set from the first ISO sample; this report refreshes the
numbers, extends the evidence to the IEV corpus, and confirms or
adjusts the calibration;

* *EFS 2525* (concept arrangement): its arrangement requirements —
criteria of subdivision, MECE discipline, cross-system membership —
respond to the arrangement-level findings of clauses <<iso-findings>>
and <<comparison>>; and

* *EFG 2519* (register operation guide): its cadence and monitoring
practice consumes the era and defect-rate profiles as the benchmark
that a conforming register is measured against.

The report is also a demonstration of method. The ES series asserts
that registers be machine-readable because machine-readable records
can be measured; this report is what measurement looks like, and the
reproduction instructions of Annex E are the standing offer that the
same measurement can be run over any conforming register — including
the registers ES itself operates.

=== How the report was produced

The review was carried out by the Terminology Committee as part of
the EFS 2522 calibration cycle. The analysis scripts are
single-pass implementations of the rules of Annex C over the
extracted corpora; the report text and the statistics were produced
in the same cycle, and the figures in this edition were generated
on 13 November 2026. The corpus states, the seeds and the protocol
of Annex E make the edition reproducible; deviations introduced by
future extractions are attributable to the corpus states, not to
the method.

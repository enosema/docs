== Foreword

ES is the publishing imprint of the Enosema Foundation. ES documents

This report was prepared by Technical Committee Terminology.

This report is an informative document of the ES Terminology series
in the document class *report* (EFD 102): it reports the findings of a
study, states the methods of the study, and reaches conclusions. It
does not contain requirements; the requirements that the findings
inform are stated in the normative documents of the series — EFS 2513
(concept management systems), EFS 2522 (concept entry quality), and
EFS 2525 (concept arrangement in concept systems).

The subject of the study is the vocabulary that the international
standardization system actually produces and maintains. Two corpora
were analysed:

* the terminology published through the ISO Online Browsing Platform
(OBP), extracted in machine-readable form and analysed through a
sample of 4 662 localized concept entries drawn from 406 898 extracted
records; and

* the IEC International Electrotechnical Vocabulary (IEV) as
maintained by IEC TC 1 in Glossarist form, analysed through a sample
of 2 000 concept records carrying 23 936 language entries.

All statistics in this report derive from those samples. Annex E
states the reproduction instructions, including the sample seeds, so
that every figure in this report can be regenerated from the corpora.

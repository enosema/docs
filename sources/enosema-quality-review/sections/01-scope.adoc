== Scope

This report reports the findings of a quality review of international
standard vocabulary. It covers:

* the composition of the two corpora studied: the ISO Online Browsing
Platform terminology extraction and the IEC International
Electrotechnical Vocabulary records maintained by IEC TC 1 (clause
<<corpora>>);

* the methodology of the review: the sampling design, the measures,
and the operational detection rules from which every statistic
derives (clauses <<methodology>> and <<detection-rules>>);

* the findings for each corpus: presence and length statistics,
structural discipline, entry element coverage, era profiles, and
document and language concentration (clauses <<iso-findings>> and
<<iev-findings>>);

* the comparison of the two systems of maintenance and the two
localization models they embody (clause <<comparison>>);

* the census of anti-patterns — the recurring defect classes that the
ES quality framework names (clause <<anti-patterns>>); and

* the implications of the findings for the normative documents of the
ES Terminology series (clause <<implications>>).

This report is informative. It states findings and conclusions; it
does not state requirements. The requirement documents that consume
its findings — EFS 2513, EFS 2522, EFS 2525, and EFG 2519 — carry
the normative text, and clause <<implications>> traces each finding
to the requirements it informs.

The review covers the corpora as they stood at extraction. The ISO
Online Browsing Platform changes continuously; the IEV records
change with TC 1 maintenance. The figures are dated by the extraction
dates of clause <<corpora>> and are reproducible from the corpora by
the instructions of Annex E.

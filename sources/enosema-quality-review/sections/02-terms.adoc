[[terms-report]]
== Terms and conventions

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this report, the following terms and definitions
apply. The report uses the terms of the ES Terminology series as
defined in EFS 2510, EFS 2513 and EFS 2522; the entries below fix the
measurement senses in which this report uses them.

[[m-entry]]
==== localized concept entry

record of one concept in one language, comprising its designations
and, where the entry carries them, its definition, notes, examples,
and sources

[NOTE: In the ISO corpus, a localized concept entry is one language
block of one Glossarist concept record. In the IEV corpus, it is one
language key of one concept file. The unit of analysis throughout is
the localized concept entry unless a clause states otherwise.]

[[m-defining-entry]]
==== definition-bearing entry

localized concept entry whose definition field carries a non-empty
definition

[[m-term-only-entry]]
==== term-only entry

localized concept entry that carries designations and no definition

[[m-definition-length]]
==== definition length

count of whitespace-separated tokens in the concatenation of the
definition text of a definition-bearing entry

[NOTE: Stem expressions, markup and punctuation are counted as
tokens. The measure overstates length for symbolic definitions and
understates it for prose; clause <<detection-rules>> states the rule
verbatim.]

[[m-defect]]
==== defect

property of a localized concept entry that a detection rule of clause
<<detection-rules>> evaluates as present

[NOTE: Defects are measured, not judged: a defect is whatever the
stated rule detects, and the rule is part of the finding. Rates are
comparable only through the rules.]

[[m-era]]
==== document era

assignment of a source document to one of three periods by its latest
year of publication: legacy (before 1998), transitional (1998 to
2014), and modern (2015 and later)

[NOTE: The periods track the regime changes of the drafting rules:
the pre-10241-1 practice, the Directives Part 2 consolidation, and
the current regime.]

=== Conventions

* Percentages are rounded to one decimal place unless a clause states
otherwise; sums may therefore differ from 100 by rounding.

* The word *corpus* refers to the full extracted collection; the word
*sample* refers to the analysed subset drawn from it (clause
<<methodology>>).

* Sample seeds are stated so that every figure regenerates exactly
(Annex E).

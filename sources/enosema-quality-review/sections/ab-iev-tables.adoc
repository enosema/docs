[[b-russian-residue]]
=== The Russian residue

Russian is the only language whose entries carry definitions
besides the authoritative pair: 21 of 190 entries (11,1 %), median
15 words. Under the declared term-only model for Russian, these
definitions are anomalies of the record set — most plausibly the
residue of a partial definition-authoring effort. The review
records them without removal: the corpus is reported as extracted,
and the anomaly is a finding about the record set's history.

[[b-notes-distribution]]
=== Notes distribution across models

Every notes-bearing entry in the sample carries an authoritative
language code: 642 English and 643 French entries, 1 285 in total,
with zero notes in the term-only layer. The note element belongs to
the definition-bearing model in this corpus, entirely: a note
written against a definition has no carrier in a designation-only
record, and the maintenance system produces none. The finding
sharpens the model contrast of clause <<comparison>> — under the
term-only model the annotation culture of the authoritative
languages does not leak outward at all.

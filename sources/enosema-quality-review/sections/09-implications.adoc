[[implications]]
== Implications for the ES Terminology series

This clause states what the findings mean for the normative
documents of the series. The statements are informative
conclusions; the normative texts carry the requirements.

=== For EFS 2522 (concept entry quality)

* *The length anchors hold.* The corpus medians — 16 words (ISO),
18 and 19 (IEV eng/fra) — sit inside the scoring anchors that EFS
2522 sets for definition length. No recalibration is indicated;
the report refreshes the anchor evidence.

* *The substitution test is justified.* A 3,5 % circularity rate in
the largest vocabulary production system, falling but persistent,
is the case for keeping the substitution test as a mandatory-fix
criterion rather than a scored nicety.

* *The example criterion is aspirational and should stay so.* At
2,9 % and 0,1 %, current international practice does not produce
examples. EFS 2522 scores examples as a completeness criterion; the
score weight is correct precisely because the corpus shows the gap
— a conforming termbase is meant to out-produce the corpus here,
and the scoring gives it credit for doing so.

* *The article-head criterion stays.* The era-flat rate marks a
habit; a habit responds to review, and the clarity criteria of
EFS 2522 review it.

* *Grammar fields need an evidence source.* Both corpora are
silent on grammar in their extracted record formats. EFS 2522
requires grammar information; the requirement rests on ISO
12620-class practice rather than on these corpora, and the next
review cycle should seek a corpus that carries grammar fields
before adjusting any anchor.

=== For EFS 2525 (concept arrangement)

* *Criteria are the gap.* The corpora exhibit rakes whose criteria
are implicit; the requirement to record the criterion of
subdivision on every membership relation (EFS 2525, A-2, Q-2) is
the response to the largest arrangement-level silence the corpora
show.

* *Residual discipline is needed at scale.* The "`other`"-class
node appears across the corpora at every depth. The residual
category rule of EFS 2525 (M-6) — declare, record as limitation,
prefer positive criteria — is the discipline the corpus practice
lacks.

* *Cross-system reality.* The IEV's tiered language coverage and
the ISO corpus's overlapping document vocabularies show that
concepts live in several systems. The EFS 2525 cross-system
requirements (A-9 to A-13) formalize what the corpora do
informally.

=== For EFG 2519 (register operation guide)

* *The benchmarks are set.* A conforming register compares its
defect rates against the era profiles: empty definitions below the
modern-era 0,2 %, circularity below 2,6 %, and example provision
above the corpus rate are the measurable targets the guide's
monitoring practice consumes.

* *The declared-model doctrine is confirmed.* The IEV operates both
localization models in one vocabulary and demonstrates the failure
mode of each; the guide's requirement to declare one model per
register and review for its failure mode is the operational
lesson of clause <<comparison>>.

=== For the series as a whole

Three conclusions close the review:

* *Enforcement beats exhortation.* Every corpus behaviour that a
process gates (sources, term type, definition presence in the
modern era) is at or near uniform; every behaviour left to author
initiative (examples, notes) lapses. The ES series gates
publication (EFS 2513, clause 10) and scores entries (EFS 2522,
clause 11) because gating and scoring move the rates that manuals
cannot.

* *Machine-readable records make quality measurable.* This review
exists because two vocabularies publish structured records. Every
requirement of the series that pushes registers toward published
machine-readable records is also a requirement that makes the
next edition of this review deeper — the registry becomes the
corpus.

* *The review is a cycle.* The census names four patterns this
cycle could not measure; the corpora age; the series changes
practice and the practice changes the corpora. The next review
cycle repeats the method of clause <<methodology>> over refreshed
extractions and over ES's own registers, and the reproduction
instructions of Annex E are written for exactly that reuse.

=== Stewardship of the evidence base

The corpora that made this review possible are maintained by others,
and the ES series holds them as an entrusted instrument:

* *the extractions are cited by state.* Every edition of this
review names its corpus states — extraction dates, record counts —
so that the communities maintaining the platforms can see exactly
what was measured and correct the record where the extraction
misreads their published content;

* *the analysis gives back.* The detection rules of Annex C and the
reproduction protocol of Annex E are offered to the corpus
communities: a committee that adopts the rules can measure its own
vocabulary with the instrument this review used, and compare
against the published baselines on equal terms; and

* *the next cycle is scheduled with the series.* The review
repeats when the corpora change materially or when the series
publishes requirements whose calibration needs new evidence,
whichever comes first; the coverage gaps of this cycle are the
agenda of the next.

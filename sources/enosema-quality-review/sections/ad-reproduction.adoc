== Reproduction

This annex states how every figure in this report regenerates from
the corpora. The analysis is a set of scripts run over the extracted
corpora; the seeds and the corpus states are the two things a
reproduction holds fixed.

The ISO extraction and the IEV record set are held in the ES
research environment. The corpora are third-party content; the
extraction and the analysis are internal to ES, and the report
publishes statistics only.

=== Sample seeds

* ISO: `random.sample(records, 3000)` with `random.seed(2522)`,
analysing all localized concept entries in the sampled records —
4 662 entries in this corpus state.

* IEV: `random.sample(sorted(records), 2000)` with
`random.seed(2522)` — 2 000 concepts, 23 936 entries.

The seed value 2522 is the document number of EFS 2522, whose first
calibration cycle introduced the analysis. Future cycles may change
seeds; the seed in force is stated with the edition of this report.

=== Measures implementation

Each rule of Annex C is a direct implementation over the parsed
records:

* definition presence and length: rules R1 and R2 over the
definition field of each language entry;

* circularity, requirements language, article head: rules R3 to R5
over the definition text;

* presence measures: rules R6 to R9 over the corresponding fields;
and

* era and language measures: rules R10 to R12 over the record
metadata.

The implementations are intentionally single-pass and dependency-
free: a reviewer with the corpus directory, a YAML parser and the
rules of Annex C reproduces every rate without the original tooling.

=== Regeneration protocol

A regeneration for a future edition of this report proceeds:

. refresh the extractions from the live platforms and record the
extraction dates and record counts;

. re-run the sampling with the seed in force and record the sample
sizes;

. re-apply the measures of Annex C, unchanged unless a rule change
is documented in the edition;

. compare the new rates with the rates of this edition, with the
sampling error of <<a-sampling-error>> as the yardstick; and

. state the rule changes, the corpus state changes and the rate
changes in the new edition, so that every difference is attributable.

=== Known coverage gaps for the next cycle

The reproduction protocol carries the review's own improvement list:

* circularity scoring for the IEV authoritative definitions
(clause <<comparison>>, gap);

* grammar-information measurement from a record format that carries
grammar fields (both corpora lack it as extracted);

* anti-patterns AP-09, AP-10 and AP-12 (clause <<anti-patterns>>),
which need document-context and arrangement-level tooling; and

* a definition-bearing multilingual corpus beyond two languages,
for the equivalence-model evidence that clause <<comparison>>
names as missing.

== Introduction

ES is committed to expanding the realm of standards for terminology,
concepts, and their management, with a focus on terminology resources
and interoperability across the globe and communities
of practice.

To facilitate its mission, the Enosema Board of Directors has decided
to align standardization and publication processes with international
best practices, such as the ISO standardization process
(<<ISODIR1>>, <<ISODIR1C>>, <<ISODIR2>>)
and the <<WTOTBT>>.

This document aims to achieve the said intention.

The document describes requirements for ES standard documents.

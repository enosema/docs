[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

[[term-contribution]]
=== contribution

material provided as input to an ES standardization activity

NOTE: a contribution can be provided by a member of ES or an external party


=== contributor

party that provides a _contribution_ (<<term-contribution>>)

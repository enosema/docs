[[bulk-exports]]
== Bulk exports

=== Required formats

A published dataset shall offer bulk exports in at least:

. **CSV**, one row per concept per language, with columns covering the
term identifier, URN, status, language, preferred term, alternative
terms, definition, notes, examples, and source references;

. **TTL** (Turtle), the SKOS projection per <<skos-projection>>; and

. **JSON-LD**, the concept records as linked data.

=== Optional formats

A publisher may additionally offer: TBX (per ISO 30042), JSON Lines, and
YAML exports. Where offered, optional exports shall carry the same
concept set as the required exports: no format shall silently drop
concepts, localizations, or relations that another format carries.

=== Consistency

All bulk exports of a dataset edition shall be generated from the same
snapshot of the dataset: a consumer combining exports from one edition
can assume cross-format consistency of identifiers and content.

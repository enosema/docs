== Publication profile

=== General

A browsable publication of a dataset — a register site — is a consumer
of the formats of this document. This clause specifies the requirements
a publication shall meet so that the published site faithfully presents
the dataset.

=== Faithfulness requirements

The publication shall preserve and present, at minimum:

. designations with their type and normative status, distinguishing
preferred from admitted and deprecated forms;

. definitions, notes, and examples, including embedded figures where the
dataset references them: an image reference present in a definition,
note, or example shall render as an image, not as literal text;

. the language set of each concept, with language display order per the
dataset's `language_order`;

. relations: typed and directed per EFS 2510, including inverse
presentation where helpful for reading; and

. sources, with links where the record carries them.

=== Identification

Each concept page shall expose the concept's stable identifier and URN,
and shall resolve at a stable URL derived from them. The site shall
offer the bulk exports of <<bulk-exports>> at stable URLs, so that
machine consumers can fetch datasets without scraping pages.

=== Search

A publication shall provide search across designations and definitions
of the dataset. Where the site publishes more than one dataset edition,
search shall distinguish editions, and superseded editions shall be
presentable but marked as such per the register metadata status.

=== Build-time preservation

Fields present in the source dataset shall reach the published site:
build tooling shall not drop model fields before rendering. A
publication pipeline that discards fields (for example, dropping
annotations or sources during an intermediate conversion) is
nonconforming even if the final pages look correct, because downstream
consumers of the pipeline's output lose the data.

NOTE: The concept-browser toolchain is the reference implementation of
this publication profile.

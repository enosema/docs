[[datasets]]
== Datasets, register metadata and packaging

=== Dataset directory

A dataset is a directory containing:

. `register.yaml`, the register metadata file (<<register-metadata>>);

. the concept record files (<<concept-records>>); and

. optionally, per-language about pages, a bibliography file, and image
assets referenced by definitions.

Dataset directories shall be consumable from a local path or a packaged
archive (<<gcr-package>>) interchangeably: consumers shall not depend on
files or layout beyond the ones this document specifies.

[[register-metadata]]
=== Register metadata

The `register.yaml` file shall carry:

. `schema_type` equal to `glossarist` and `schema_version` equal to `3`;

. `id`, the dataset identifier, unique within its publication;

. `ref`, the human-readable reference of the dataset;

. `year`, the edition year;

. `urn`, the URN identifying the dataset as a whole;

. `status`, the lifecycle status of the dataset edition (for example,
`current`, `superseded`);

. `owner`, the organization responsible for the dataset;

. `source_repo`, the canonical repository of the source data; and

. `languages` and `language_order`, the ISO 639-3 codes of the dataset's
localizations in their display priority.

Additional fields (tags, sections, ordering) may be carried; consumers
shall preserve unknown fields rather than discard them.

[[gcr-package]]
=== GCR package

A GCR package is the packaged form of a dataset: an archive containing
the dataset directory's contents, published at a stable URL for
download by consumers. A package shall be versioned by its release, and
a package shall not change after publication: corrections produce a new
package release.

Datasets published as GCR packages shall remain byte-identical in
content to their source dataset directories.

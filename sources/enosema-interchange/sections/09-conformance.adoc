[[conformance]]
== Conformance

=== Requirements classes

[[req-producer]]
==== Concept record producer

A system claiming the producer class shall emit concept records
conforming to <<concept-records>> and dataset metadata conforming to
<<register-metadata>>, and shall not drop fields it does not understand
when re-emitting records produced by others.

[[req-publisher]]
==== Dataset publisher

A system claiming the publisher class shall satisfy the producer class
and shall additionally provide the bulk exports of <<bulk-exports>>, a
SKOS projection conforming to <<skos-projection>> where SKOS
publication is offered, and GCR packages per <<gcr-package>> where
packaged distribution is offered.

[[req-site]]
==== Publication site

A system claiming the publication-site class shall satisfy the
publication profile of <<publication>>, consuming conforming datasets
without modifying their records.

=== Conformance claims

A conformance claim shall state the class(es) claimed, the tool or
system assessed, and its version. Claims shall be checkable from the
formats alone: conformance to this document does not depend on
undocumented behavior of any implementation.

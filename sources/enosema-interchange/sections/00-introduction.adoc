== Introduction

Concept records are only as useful as they are interchangeable. A concept
created under the requirements of EFS 2513, expressed in the vocabulary of
EFS 2510, and localized per EFS 2504 must survive the trip between
systems: authoring tools, registers, publication frontends, and
downstream consumers — human and machine.

Today that trip happens ad hoc. Every tool invents its own field names,
its own status vocabulary, its own packaging. Records are exported and
re-imported with silent field loss; SKOS projections become the only
surviving representation; identifiers change between systems.

This document specifies the interchange of terminology and concept data
for ES purposes. It defines:

* the concept record format for interchange, based on the glossarist v3
dataset model (managed concept and localized concept as separate,
cross-referenced records);

* dataset identity and packaging, including the register metadata file
and the GCR package container;

* the SKOS projection profile: exactly how ES concept systems project
into SKOS, and which information such a projection is permitted to lose;

* bulk export requirements and the publication profile for browsable
publication of registers.

The reference implementations of this document are the ES concept
register (the relation vocabulary of EFS 2510, published at the Enosema
concept register) and the concept-browser publication toolchain. Where
this document specifies a format, those implementations demonstrate it;
where a requirement is stated with *shall*, conformance is checkable
against them.

[[concept-records]]
== Concept record interchange

=== Record layout

A concept is interchanged as one YAML file containing two documents:

. the *managed concept* record: the concept's identifier, URN, lifecycle
status, acceptance dates, sources, relations, and the localized-concept
map; and

. one *localized concept* record per language: the language code, terms
with type and normative status, definitions, notes, examples, and
sources.

The managed record and each localized record are cross-referenced by
identifier: the managed record's localized-concept map keys language
codes to localized record identifiers.

[source,yaml]
----
---
id: my-concept-01
data:
  identifier: my-concept-01
  uri: urn:example:concept:my-concept-01
  localized_concepts:
    eng: eng-my-concept-01
status: valid
date_accepted: '2026-01-01T00:00:00+00:00'
sources:
- type: authoritative
  origin:
    ref:
      source: Example Register
---
id: eng-my-concept-01
termid: my-concept-01
data:
  language_code: eng
  terms:
  - type: expression
    designation: example concept
    normative_status: preferred
  definition:
  - content: "A concept used as an interchange example."
status: valid
----

=== Field requirements

A conforming managed concept record shall carry:

. `id` and `data.identifier`, equal and stable per the identifier
scheme of EFS 2513;

. `data.uri`, a URN that resolves per the register's URI policy;

. `data.localized_concepts`, mapping ISO 639-3 language codes to
localized record identifiers;

. `status`, carrying the registration status per ISO 19135-1;

. `sources`, citing the origin of the concept.

A conforming localized concept record shall carry:

. `termid`, the identifier of its managed concept;

. `data.language_code`, an ISO 639-3 code; where a localization is
romanized or transliterated, the record shall also carry the script
(ISO 15924) and writing system conversion system (ISO 24229) fields per
EFS 2504;

. at least one `terms` entry with `designation`, `type` and
`normative_status`; and

. `definition` entries whose `content` states the intension of the
concept.

=== Relations

Concept relations shall be carried on the managed concept record as
typed references per EFS 2510: each relation records the relation type
identifier, the related concept reference, and optional explanatory
content. N-ary relations are interchanged as relation records per
EFS 2510, as files separate from the concept records, and shall not be
encoded as binary relations for the convenience of any tool.

=== Scalar quoting

Interchange files are YAML. Scalars that contain colons, quotes, or
other YAML-delimiting characters shall be quoted; unquoted scalars that
a YAML parser reads as a different type or structure than the record
intends are a conformance error of the producing system.

NOTE: Definitions citing standards, for example ISO 704:2022, contain
colon characters and are a recurring source of such errors.

== Support

=== Resources

The organization shall determine and provide the resources needed for the
concept management system, including:

. the staffing of the roles of <<governance>>;

. the tooling for authoring, reviewing, validating, and publishing
concept records; and

. the infrastructure for publication and for machine access.

=== Competence and awareness

The organization shall ensure that persons performing concept work are
competent in the terminology methods this document relies on, including
ISO 704 concept analysis, definition drafting per ISO 10241-1, and the
relation vocabulary in use.

The organization shall ensure that all participants are aware of:

. the concept management policy (<<governance>>);

. their roles and the role boundaries; and

. the consequences of nonconforming changes to published concepts.

=== Documented information

The organization shall maintain as controlled records:

. the concept management policy and governance decisions;

. the concept system scopes, criteria of subdivision, and identifier
scheme;

. the concept records, relation records, and localization records; and

. the history of every record: what changed, when, and by whose decision.

=== Machine-readable records

The organization shall publish concept records in a machine-readable form
that preserves, at minimum:

. identifiers, designations, definitions, and sources;

. relation type identifiers, directions, and n-ary metadata per EFS 2510;

. localization fields per <<localization-mgmt>>; and

. lifecycle statuses.

Where records are published as linked data, the organization should use
SKOS for interoperable projection while retaining the full record per
EFS 2510.

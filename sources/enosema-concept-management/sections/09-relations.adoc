[[relations-mgmt]]
== Relation management

=== Controlled vocabulary

The organization shall manage concept relations against a controlled
relation vocabulary. The organization shall:

. adopt the relation vocabulary of EFS 2510, or a registered domain
profile derived from it;

. record, for each relation, the relation type identifier, direction, and
relevant metadata per EFS 2510; and

. treat relation type identifiers not present in the adopted vocabulary as
errors at validation, not as data to be preserved silently.

=== System-level relation rules

The organization shall apply, at minimum, the structural rules of
EFS 2510 to every concept system it manages:

* hierarchical relations are acyclic;

* every subdivision states its criterion of subdivision;

* coordinate concepts within one subdivision are delimited from one
another;

* n-ary relations are recorded as first-class items with members,
completeness, and criterion; and

* lifecycle relations link records so that retirement and replacement
remain traceable.

=== Relations across organizations

Where concepts relate to concepts managed by another organization, the
organization shall record the relation using the mapping relation types
(`exact_match`, `close_match`, `broad_match`, `narrow_match`,
`related_match`) and shall identify the other vocabulary in the relation
record. The organization shall not assert `equivalent` across
vocabularies it does not control; equivalence within its own system
remains available.

=== Review of relations

Relation records shall be included in the periodic review of concept
systems (<<evaluation>>), with attention to:

. dangling or cyclic references;

. missing inverses where inverses are materialized; and

. lifecycle relations recorded without their register counterparts.

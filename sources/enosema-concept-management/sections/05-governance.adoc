[[governance]]
== Governance, roles and responsibilities

=== Management commitment

Top management shall demonstrate commitment to the concept management
system by:

. ensuring that the concept management policy and objectives are
established and compatible with the strategic direction of the
organization;

. ensuring the availability of resources (<<support>>); and

. appointing the control body (<<term-control-body>>) and the register
manager (<<term-register-manager>>) where a register is operated.

=== Concept management policy

The organization shall establish a concept management policy that:

. states the principles governing concept creation, definition, review,
and change in the organization;

. commits to the stability and citability of published concepts;

. commits to machine-readable records and to the vocabularies this suite
of ES specifications and the cited ISO standards define; and

. is communicated to all parties performing work under the system.

=== Governance body

The organization shall establish a control body with the authority to:

. accept, reject, or return proposals to create, revise, split, merge, or
retire concepts;

. resolve conflicts between concept owners and between consumers; and

. decide appeals against its own decisions through a defined appeals
process.

The decisions of the control body shall be recorded. The appeals process
shall be documented and shall specify who hears an appeal and within what
time.

=== Roles

The organization shall assign the following roles, whether as persons or
as bodies:

* *concept owner* (<<term-concept-owner>>) for each concept or coherent
set of concepts: accountable for the intension, extension, and lifecycle
of the concepts;

* *register manager* where a register is operated: accountable for record
integrity, registration procedure execution, and publication;

* *submitters*: parties entitled to propose items, drawn from the
membership or participation rules the organization defines;

* *reviewers*: parties tasked with the technical review of proposals.

The assignment of roles shall be recorded. A person may hold several
roles, except that the register manager shall not be the sole reviewer of
their own proposals.

=== Participation

The organization shall document who may participate in concept work, and
on what terms, including:

. the membership classes that confer proposal and voting rights;

. the conditions under which external parties may participate; and

. the handling of contributions from parties bound by confidentiality,
which shall not be accepted for concepts whose records are public.

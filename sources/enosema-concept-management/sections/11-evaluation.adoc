== Evaluation

=== Monitoring

The organization shall monitor the health of its concept management
system, including:

. the volume and throughput of proposals and their decision times;

. the number and severity of validation errors in published records; and

. consumer-reported issues with concepts, references, and localizations.

=== Periodic review of concept systems

The organization shall review each concept system at planned intervals.
The review shall verify:

. the constraints of EFS 2510 against the live system, with errors
logged and dispositioned;

. that every subdivision still carries its criterion of subdivision;

. that lifecycle events are recorded on both the register and the concept
records where both exist;

. the continued validity of equivalence degrees for localizations affected
by source changes (<<localization-mgmt>>); and

. that retired records remain resolvable and carry successor information.

=== Internal audit

The organization shall audit its concept management system at planned
intervals to verify conformity with this document. The audit shall cover
the governance decisions, the records of the selected period, and the
operation of the registration and change procedures.

Audit criteria, scope, and findings shall be recorded. Nonconformities
shall be dispositioned through <<improvement>>.

=== Conformity claims

An organization claiming conformity to this document shall state:

. the requirements class claimed (whole document or a class of
Annex <<req-classes>>);

. the concept systems and registers in scope of the claim; and

. the date and basis of the assessment on which the claim rests.

[[terms]]
== Terms, definitions and abbreviations

=== Terms and definitions

For the purposes of this document, the following terms and definitions apply.

[[term-concept-management-system]]
==== concept management system
alt:[CMS]

set of governance arrangements, processes, and records by which an
organization manages the concepts for which it is responsible

[[term-concept-owner]]
==== concept owner

party accountable for the intension, extension, and lifecycle of a concept
or a defined set of concepts

[[term-register-manager]]
==== register manager

party responsible for the operation of a concept register, including the
integrity of its records and the execution of its registration procedures

[[term-control-body]]
==== control body

body that decides on the acceptance, revision, and retirement of
registered items under the registration procedures

[SOURCE: ISO 19135-1, modified]

[[term-submitting-organization]]
==== submitting organization

party that proposes an item for registration

[SOURCE: ISO 19135-1]

[[term-concept-record]]
==== concept record

structured description of one concept, comprising its designations,
definitions, relations, sources, and management metadata

[[term-concept-system-scope]]
==== concept system scope

statement of the domain, purpose, and boundaries of the concepts managed
by an organization

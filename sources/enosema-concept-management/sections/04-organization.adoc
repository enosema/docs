[[organization]]
== Organization and its context

=== Understanding the organization and its context

The organization shall determine the external and internal issues that
bear on its purpose and on the concepts it manages, including:

. the domains whose concepts it manages, and the domains that depend on it;

. the regulatory, contractual, or interoperability obligations attached to
those concepts;

. the users and consumers of its concepts, human and machine; and

. the lifecycle of the systems that consume its concepts.

=== Scope of the concept management system

The organization shall define a concept system scope
(<<term-concept-system-scope>>) stating:

. the domains and subject fields covered;

. the purposes the concepts serve (standardization, regulation,
interoperability, reference data, publication); and

. the boundaries: concepts explicitly excluded, and the registers with
which the system interworks.

The concept system scope shall be maintained as a controlled record and
shall be reviewed when the organization's context changes.

=== Consumers and their requirements

The organization shall identify the consumers of its concepts — including
downstream systems, translations, and other registers — and shall
determine their requirements for stability, citability, traceability, and
format of concept records.

The organization shall ensure that changes to its concepts are assessed
against these requirements before being applied (<<change-mgmt>>).

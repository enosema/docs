== Improvement

=== Nonconformity and corrective action

When a nonconformity is identified — through validation, review, audit,
or consumer report — the organization shall:

. correct the nonconforming records;

. determine whether the cause lies in the records, the process, or the
governance, and act on the cause; and

. review whether similar nonconformities exist elsewhere in the system.

=== Continual improvement

The organization shall improve the suitability of its concept management
system through:

. the outcomes of monitoring and review (<<evaluation>>);

. changes in consumer requirements (<<organization>>);

. evolution of the vocabularies and registers this document relies on,
including new versions of EFS 2510 and of the ISO 24229 register entries
used in localization; and

. decisions of the control body recorded as improvements to the system.

The organization shall record the improvement decisions and verify their
effect at the next review.

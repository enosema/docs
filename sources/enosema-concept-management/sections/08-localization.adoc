[[localization-mgmt]]
== Localization management

=== General

Where the organization publishes concepts in more than one language, the
localization of concepts is a governed process, not an incidental
translation. The requirements of this clause supplement EFS 2504.

=== Localization records

For each localization, the organization shall record:

. the language of the localization as an ISO 639-3 three-letter code;

. the script as an ISO 15924 code, wherever the language is rendered in
more than one script or the localization is a romanization or
transliteration; and

. the writing system conversion system as an ISO 24229 register item,
wherever the localization is produced by conversion between writing
systems, citing the ISO 24229 identifier of the conversion system
(for example, `urn:iso:24229:system-code:...`).

A localization produced by conversion shall carry both the source and the
target script, and the ISO 24229 conversion system used. The organization
shall not record a conversion system identifier that is not registered in
the ISO 24229 register.

=== Equivalence governance

The organization shall govern cross-language equivalence per ISO 704 and
EFS 2504:

. each localization shall carry an equivalence degree: `full`, `partial`,
`none`, or `directional`;

. a localization with degree `partial` or `directional` shall carry a
note explaining the difference; and

. acceptance of a localization with degree other than `full` shall be a
control body decision, with the reasons recorded.

=== Localization workflow

The organization shall define and apply a localization workflow that
determines:

. which localizations are required for which concepts (by policy or by
consumer need);

. who performs, reviews, and accepts localizations; and

. how localized concepts are updated when the source concept changes: a
change to a source concept shall trigger review of every localization of
that concept whose equivalence degree depends on the changed intension.

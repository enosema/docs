== Scope

This document specifies requirements for a concept management system: the
governance, processes, and records by which an organization manages the
concepts it is responsible for.

It is applicable to:

* any organization that creates, maintains, publishes, or registers
concepts, regardless of sector, size, or domain;

* concept registers operated under ISO 19135 procedures and the FERIN
framework;

* terminology collections maintained as concept systems in the sense of
ISO 704 and EFS 2501.

This document is adoptable by any organization. Conformity can be claimed
for the requirements classes of Annex <<req-classes>> as well as for the
document as a whole.

This document does not specify:

* the principles and methods of terminology work itself, which are
specified in ISO 704 and EFS 2501;

* the structure of concept systems and concept relations, which are
specified in EFS 2510;

* the management of multilingual terminology content, which is specified
in EFS 2504, beyond the localization governance requirements of
<<localization-mgmt>>;

* the design of any particular software tool.
